## Problem
The snack aisle and the deli miss today. counterProbe48 pins "chips", "crackers", "cookies", "protein bar" and "hummus" as misses, and nothing catches "tortilla chips", "popcorn", "pretzels", "trail mix", "potato salad" or "fresh pasta". The shelf choices are label and craft choices:
- multigrain versus whole grain first on a cracker or snack list;
- tortilla chips from masa versus corn chips from plain cornmeal;
- chips sliced from potatoes versus pressed from dried flakes;
- plain kernels versus a pre-flavored bag;
- the lye-dipped pretzel and its deep brown crust;
- bars led by oats versus refined grain and syrups;
- trail mix that is nuts and fruit, versus a long added list;
- hummus of chickpeas and tahini, and what the gum is for;
- deli salads bought cold and kept cold, and how many days they keep;
- a hot case held hot, with a thermometer the shopper can read;
- fresh egg pasta, its short list and its short opened life.

None of these has a card behind it, and the two broad questions ("which snacks", "what from the deli") have no broad card to name the next question.
