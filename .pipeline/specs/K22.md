## Problem
The sauce and can aisles miss today. `k21.test.js` NOT_NEW pins "soy sauce", "vinegar", "salsa" and "coconut water" as landing on no K21 id, and nothing else catches them. The shelf choices are label and craft choices:
- percent acidity, and pasteurized versus unpasteurized vinegar;
- jam or preserves made from fruit, versus jelly made from juice, and reduced sugar;
- what ketchup and prepared mustard are made of, and stone ground versus yellow mustard;
- mayonnaise versus "salad dressing", and which oil the list names first;
- soy sauce brewed for months versus made in hours with acid;
- the "low", "reduced" and "no salt added" sodium wording, and dented cans;
- olives cured in brine versus lye;
- seaweed labeled by kind;
- single-acting versus double-acting leaveners;
- salsa in a cold tub versus a heat-sealed jar;
- coconut water alone versus with added sugar;
- what a condiment's first ingredient and serving size say.

None of these has a card behind it.
