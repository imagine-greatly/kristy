- **NN2.** Every claim traces to a file named in step 1's table, with no new number or concern beyond the archive. - **NN8.** No price, and nothing relative to price.
- **NN9.** No brand and no store. Brands named inside cited files never reach the prose: Tasting Table and chowhound name brands and stores.

- **Source preference:** regulatory (USC, CFR, TTB, FDA CPG), then extension and Wikipedia, then food press. A commercial page is never cited for a claim.
