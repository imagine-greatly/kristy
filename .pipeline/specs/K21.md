## Problem
The pantry staples on nearly every grocery list miss today: salt, sugar, broth, cooking oil, cocoa, tea, seeds, dried fruit. `counterProbe48.test.js:57` pins "maple syrup" as a miss. The shelf choices are label choices:
- iodized or not, and grain;
- white versus brown sugar, and what "brown" means;
- pure maple syrup versus table syrup, and grade color;
- refined versus unrefined oil;
- lard hardened with hydrogenated lard or not;
- natural versus alkalized cocoa;
- sulfites and added sugar on dried fruit;
- fortified versus unfortified nutritional yeast;
- gums in canned coconut milk;
- irradiation and "spice" on dried herbs;
- decaffeinated versus herbal on tea.

None of these has a card behind it.
