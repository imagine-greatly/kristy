Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Working version. This policy accurately describes how Kristy Approved handles your information today, and it applies now. It is pending a full legal review, and we will update this page when that review is complete.

Kristy Approved (“Kristy”, “we”, “us”) is a grocery-coaching app. It reads packaged food labels, answers questions about the counters that have no barcode, and helps you build a shopping list. This page explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and what you can do about it.

Phone numbers and SMS messages

What we collect and why

We collect your mobile phone number so we can send you a one-time verification code (OTP) by SMS to sign you in to your account. Kristy has no passwords: the code is how you prove the account is yours.

How you opt in

Entering your phone number on the sign-in screen and requesting a code constitutes your consent to receive a one-time verification code by SMS at that number. No other consent is bundled into it, and you are not enrolled in any marketing or promotional messaging by signing in.

Message frequency

One message per sign-in request. You receive a verification code only when you ask for one. We do not send recurring, promotional, or marketing messages.

How you opt out, and how to get help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out and you will receive no further SMS messages from us. Reply HELP for help, or email us at hello@kristyapproved.com. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. If you opt out you can still use the app, but you will not be able to sign in by SMS code until you opt back in.

Who delivers the messages

SMS delivery is handled on our behalf by Twilio, through our authentication provider Supabase. Your phone number and the verification code are passed to Twilio so the message can be sent. Twilio acts as our service provider and does not use your number for its own purposes.

We do not sell or share your phone number

We do not sell or share your phone number, mobile information, or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Your phone number is used solely to authenticate you and to deliver the sign-in codes you request. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing, promotional, or lead-generation purposes. The only party that receives your number is our SMS delivery provider, acting under contract, for the sole purpose of sending the code you asked for.

What else we collect

Information you choose to give us

  • Dietary preferences and goals — coaching goals, dietary focuses (for example watching sodium or added sugar), hard-line restrictions (for example no seed oils), and practical constraints (for example budget-conscious or short on time). These are optional. The app works without them.
  • Optional profile details — such as name, age, height, weight, and eating pattern, if you enter them.
  • Messages you send Kristy — so the conversation persists across your devices.

Information created as you use the app

  • Scan activity — the barcodes you scan and the products they resolve to. You can also photograph a product’s ingredients panel; that image is sent to our AI provider to transcribe the printed text, and we do not retain the photograph.
  • Cart and list activity — what you put on your list, what you take off, and which suggestions you decline, so the app can stop offering you something you already said no to.
  • Your haul — a record of your scans so the app can show your trip back to you.
  • Technical data — your IP address, used to rate-limit anonymous use and prevent abuse, and basic web analytics (pageviews and product events such as “a scan happened”).

Payment information

Paid memberships are processed by Stripe on the web and by Apple for in-app purchases on iOS. We never see or store your card number. We store only your subscription status and the identifiers we need to manage it.

Your individual behavior stays yours

What you personally buy, scan, and put on your list is used to personalize your experience and nothing else. It is never pooled into a shared dataset.

We do keep two anonymous, product-level records to improve the app for everyone: the details of products that have been scanned, and the text of counter questions our knowledge base could not answer well, so we know what to write next. Neither carries any user identifier — no account, no phone number, no IP address — and neither can be traced back to you. This separation is enforced in our code, not just by policy.

Sensitive information

Some of what you may tell us — dietary restrictions and health-adjacent goals — could be considered sensitive under some privacy laws. We collect it only if you choose to provide it, use it only to personalize what the app shows you, never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never pool it into the anonymous datasets described above. It is deleted when you delete your account.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed. Anonymous product and question records contain no personal information and are retained indefinitely. Payment records are retained as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything held against it at any time from inside the app — open Settings and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, goals, list and list patterns, haul, chat history, and sign-in record. It is immediate and cannot be undone. You can also email hello@kristyapproved.com and we will remove it for you.

Security

Your data is stored with row-level security, so one account cannot read another's. Card details never reach our servers. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

Children

Kristy Approved is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Kristy is not a medical provider

Kristy provides general grocery and nutrition guidance and is not a licensed dietitian, doctor, or medical provider. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet, especially if you have a health condition. If you have a food allergy, always read the physical label — do not rely on this app for allergen safety.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to how we handle your data, we will update this page and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or about SMS messages? Email hello@kristyapproved.com.
