The whole store

The store has two halves. A scanner reads the boxes. Nobody reads the counter: the meat, the fish, the eggs, the produce, the bulk bins.

Kristy reads both.

Ask the counter

Type it the way you would say it.

"Wild or farmed salmon." "Which cut for stew." "What pasture-raised actually leaves out."

The call comes back in one line, the reason in one line, and a short checklist for the case in front of you. Produce, meat, seafood, dairy and eggs, pantry and bulk, and the label terms that sit on all of them.

One card, one call

The call, the reason, the better pick.

Every claim traces back to a sourced entry. No concern appears that a sourced entry did not give, and the card says where its call stands: settled science, credible concern, or Kristy's own standard.

The better pick lands on your list in one tap.

Build the list

Say what the week is.

"Two dinners, nothing with seafood, something for lunches."

The list builds itself, sorts by section, and carries the one note that matters on each row. Change your mind in the same box and it rewrites around what you already have.

Shop mode

Walk the store without losing your place.

One section at a time, full screen, with the instruction in the largest type on the phone. Tick things off as you go. Scan something and it acts on the list in front of you.

And the rest of the trip

The packaged half, and the trip home.

Scan the boxes

Point at a barcode, or photograph an ingredient list, and Kristy reads all of it, down to the six-point type at the bottom. The seal is earned, not handed out.

Read the haul

The haul shows what you picked up and what you left behind, and carries it into next week.

Teaches the label

No brand gets run down. Kristy teaches the label instead, which never goes stale and makes you better at every product on the shelf.

What Kristy will not do

A coach, not a tracker.

  • No calorie counting, no macros, no meal logging. Nothing to weigh and nothing to confess to.
  • Not a doctor. Food is never sold as a treatment for anything. Anything medical goes to your doctor.
  • No invented concerns. Every claim traces back to a sourced entry in the knowledge base.
