What Kristy Approved is

Kristy Approved is a grocery-coaching app. It reads packaged food labels by barcode or photo and gives a graded verdict on the ingredients; it answers questions about the parts of the store that have no barcode — the butcher, the fish counter, produce, dairy, and bulk — from a sourced knowledge base; and it helps you build and keep a shopping list.

A free tier is available. A paid membership adds personalized verdict notes, a cart shaped around your goals and constraints, a read on your haul, and conversational cart edits.
