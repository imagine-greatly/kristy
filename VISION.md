# VISION — Kristy as a Presence, Not a Tool

The direction for what Kristy becomes after the mechanics are solid. Not a feature list — a character direction. The moat isn't more surfaces; it's that Kristy feels like *someone*, and you can't clone a personality.

---

## The core idea

Right now Kristy is a tool: a chat, a list, a scanner. The untapped potential is NOT horizontal (more tabs — meal planner, recipe engine, fridge tracker, community feed). That dilutes her into a Swiss Army knife. The potential is VERTICAL: more of *her* inside the three surfaces she already has.

Kristy, if she were real, is the friend who's deep into whole-food nutrition — opinionated, a little contrarian, warm but doesn't coddle, knows things you don't, has takes. Shopping with a person like that, the value isn't the list. It's *them* — their reactions, their history with you, their point of view. The relationship is the product, and it lives in voice and reaction, not features.

## The four vectors of depth

1. **Reaction, not just response.** She answers now. A presence has opinions that show up *unprompted* — a scan that delights her, an ingredient that sets her off, a pattern she noticed. The difference between a tool and a friend is that a friend volunteers.

2. **Memory that gets referenced.** Not just stored — brought up. "Third week grabbing the good yogurt." "You said you hate cooking, so —." She carries the history and mentions it. That's what makes someone feel known.

3. **A point of view on things you didn't ask about.** A weekly note that's a *thought*, not a scorecard. "Everyone's panicking about X this month — here's the honest read." This is what turns a tool into someone you *follow* — and it's the growth engine, because it's what people screenshot and send friends.

4. **Teaching that reduces dependence.** "You've gotten good at seed oils. The thing that still gets you is 'natural flavors' —." A coach whose goal is your competence is stickier, not weaker, because the *relationship* becomes the value, separate from any single verdict.

## What this is NOT

- Not more features. No meal planner, recipe app, macro tracker, social feed. Those dilute.
- Not a personality performing helpfulness (see VOICE below — that's the cringe to avoid).
- Not built before the mechanics work. A vivid character on a broken scanner is a charming liar.

## Sequencing

Build this AFTER: the list/chat/scanner loop works, the scanner identifies real products, and it's been used in a real store for a few weeks. The personality work needs a working loop to attach to, and real usage tells you which reactions actually matter. Write it down now; build it once the mechanics are solid.
