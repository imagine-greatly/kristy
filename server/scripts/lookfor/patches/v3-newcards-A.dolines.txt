cilantro	Smell the bunch, then check the leaves for yellow or brown spots.
parsley	Smell the bunch, then check the stems for wilting or yellow.
basil	Smell the bunch, then check the leaves for dark or brown spots.
cucumbers	Press along the length for soft spots; skip a slick waxed skin.
asparagus	Check the tips are closed and the spears round, not ridged.
corn	Find organic on the sign, then peel back an inch of husk.
eggplant	Lift it for weight, then check the cap for mold or browning.
zucchini	Pick the smaller, glossy ones that feel heavy in the hand.
snap_peas	Press a pod; it should feel firm, not limp.
kohlrabi	Pick the smaller globes and check for cracks.
okra	Pick short pods under four inches; skip dull or shriveled ones.
swiss_chard	Check the stalks for splits and the leaves for yellowing.
mangoes	Press gently near the stem and smell for sweetness.
celery	Squeeze the stalks for a solid feel; check the center for dark spots.
winter_squash	Lift it for weight, then check the stem is dry and whole.
microgreens	Check the pack for crisp leaves and no pooled moisture.
tofu	Read the list for soybeans, water and one coagulant.
frozen_peas	Read the bag for one ingredient, then shake it for loose peas.
frozen_broccoli	Read the bag for one ingredient, then feel for loose florets.
