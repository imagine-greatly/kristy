# Concern register

Analysis, not card copy. Built 2026-10-18 from the archives under `docs/sources/concerns/` (paths below are relative to that folder). Every row cites at least one archived file; no URL is cited from memory. Concerns are phrased as label truth, what is in the food, or how it is processed; none states that a food causes, prevents or cures anything.

Strength: **STRONG** = consumer-testing body, regulator, peer-reviewed or statutory text archived; **WEAK** = advocacy, trade or single-farm source, or a mechanism argument without testing; **COUNTER-VIEW** = an archived source disputing the concern (kept, flagged).
