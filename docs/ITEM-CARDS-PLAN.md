# Plan: item cards and one-list shop mode (2026-10-13). Direction approved by Devon 2026-10-13 ("build all of this and ship to TestFlight"); this plan is executable without a second approval round. Not built.

**Problem.** In the list and in shop mode, a card shows its **do line**, and the do line is a label-reading chore. The eggs card shows "Check the small regional cartons." when the buying rule is "Corn- and soy-free first, then organic, then pasture-raised." Produce cards say "firm, unbruised" but never which kind to buy. There is no banana card. Cards are filed by topic, not by item: eggs has six cards (egg_labels, egg_shell_color, egg_freshness, egg_grades_sizes, egg_feed_claims, egg_storage), and beef, chicken, milk and yogurt are split the same way. Shop mode pages sideways one section at a time, with a bottom bar and a tall header.
**Underlying need.** A shopper walking an aisle needs one card per thing they are buying. That card says what to put in the cart in a single glance. The label-reading detail is there if they want it.

## Decisions (made here at Rec; the admin executes them as written)
- **D1. The front is `decision`; the why line is `tier_note`; the back is `do` plus the existing depth fields.** No new field, no SQL, no change to the money boundary. iOS already renders `decision` as the card headline and `tier_note` as the free tier sentence (counter.md §4.2). `why` stays paid (DEPTH_FIELDS; Ruling F is unbuilt). What changes on iOS: list rows (cart.md §5.4) and the shop-mode lead slot (§8.2) show `decision` instead of `do`. On the card view, `do` moves below Add to cart under a "How to spot it" eyebrow. Rejected: (a) rewriting `do` as the cart rule, because DO_NOT_IMPERATIVE, OBSERVABLE_IN_BOTH and the do-lines bar all forbid restating the headline, and it would print the same rule twice on the card view; (b) an additive `front`/`why_line` column, which costs SQL, schemaContract, a migration mapping and an iOS decode for a field that `decision` already is.
  **Gate:** S1 must show that `tier_note` renders free, directly under the headline, for guests. If it does not, stop and re-dispatch the planner. Do not substitute `why`.
- **D2. The do lines stay as written.** They are the back. The do-lines bar in `docs/do-lines-review.md` is unchanged. A do line changes only when its card folds or its front rewrite trips OBSERVABLE_IN_BOTH.
- **D3. Folds are same-subject only.** A topic card folds into the item card when every one of its asks is about that item. Its aliases and asked_as move to the survivor. Its unique facts move to the survivor's back (`pick_steps`/`look_for`/`labels_decoded`) with their sources. Its id goes into RETIRED, and the entry is deleted (the chf-5 process).
  - **Never folded:** an ESSENTIALS id (the essential keeps its id and becomes the item card); a `kind='home'` technique card; a `label_*` decoder card. These keep their ids and still get a front-line pass (D5).
- **D4. Milk: the rule beats the example.** Devon's milk line, "Clean small-farm raw if you can get it; otherwise low-temp pasteurized, 100% grass-fed", is an availability retreat ("if you can get it; otherwise"). Non-negotiable #7 and HEADLINE_HEDGED send that kind of retreat to `watch_out`, and the brief says a non-negotiable outranks the approved type. "Clean" also implies safety, which the claim lock forbids. The front therefore uses rank form, which already passes lint (egg_labels' "…first, then…" is live at HEAD): **"Raw from a farm that posts its test results first, then low-temp pasteurized; 100% grass-fed either way." [PLACEHOLDER wording; 17 words, over the cap, so IC-7b shortens it, for example "Tested small-farm raw first, then low-temp pasteurized; 100% grass-fed either way." at 11 words]**. The raw-milk germ risk stays on the card as `watch_out` (accuracy outranks firmness).
- **D5. Every card in the corpus gets a front-line pass.** Section batches with folds rewrite their fronts fully. Every other card gets an audit: if its `decision` is already a cart rule in shopper words and its `tier_note` reads as a why line, it is left alone.
- **D6. Shop mode bottom bar.** It is removed. If S3 shows it holds Scan/Ask, they move to the trailing edge of the thin top line as two icon buttons (44pt hit area), because no in-store branch may be lost (cart.md §8.7). Finish trip becomes the last row of the list.

## Accuracy concerns in Devon's examples (bind the batch criteria)
1. **Bananas.** Nearly all supermarket bananas, green ones included, are ripened with ethylene in ripening rooms. No card may say or imply that yellow bananas are "chemically ripened", or that green ones are not. "Conventional is fine" may rest only on a fetched residue source; if PDP tested peeled pulp, the card says so. The front stays a cart rule. The why line is practical plus the peel fact, never a health claim. No resistant-starch line (macroGuard).
2. **Produce skin rule.** This is a heuristic, so it is `kristys_standard`, never `established`. Fetched residue data overrides it item by item, because accuracy outranks firmness. Expected exceptions: mushrooms, cabbage, asparagus and sweet corn are eaten whole but run low in residue; citrus is peeled but zested; potatoes and sweet potatoes are often eaten skin-on. Allowed wording: residue *on the food* ("USDA testing finds more residue on …"). Forbidden: exposure, risk or body effects (non-negotiable #3).
3. **Eggs.** The ladder is Devon's standard (`kristys_standard`). The live egg_feed_claims card says both "soy-free" and "corn-free" must be printed, and the fold has to keep that.
4. **Milk.** See D4. "Low-temp pasteurized" needs a fetched source (the label words are "vat pasteurized"/"low-temperature"), and raw-milk retail legality varies by state. The why line may not say that raw milk or less heat is healthier.
5. **Beef.** "100% grass-fed and grass-finished" is fine as a standard. USDA withdrew the grass-fed marketing standard in 2016, so the back names third-party seals, which the existing do line already does.

## Claim-lock path (binds IC-3 to IC-8)
1. A new sentence may only restate (i) a sentence already on this card, carrying its source verbatim; (ii) an engine KB entry; or (iii) a quote in `docs/research/item-cards-sources.md` (IC-2) or an existing archive. Builders never fetch, and never cite a URL from memory.
2. Every changed `sources` URL is grepped verbatim against the archives. Zero misses, or the piece fails.
3. `evidence_tier` stays honest: a ladder, the skin rule or a raising preference is `kristys_standard`.
4. Over changed fields, `grep -ciE "healthier|healthiest|better for you|chemically ripened|lowers|exposure"` = 0.
5. Why line (`tier_note`): one sentence, ≥5 words, not the tier name, no first person, unique in the corpus, and it comments on the front or the do line.
