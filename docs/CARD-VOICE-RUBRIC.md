# Card voice rubric (Devon-approved direction, 2026-11-01)

Applies to every shopper-facing field of a counter card. Locked rules in CLAUDE.md (claim lock, zero first person, no price, no brand claims) still bind; this is how the words get written.

## The moment
Phone in one hand, cart in the other, about 20 seconds. The item was typed at home as a broad word ("chicken"). The card answers one question: what do I grab, and how do I know it's the one?

## Two readers, one card, two layers
- **Average American (top layer).** Trusts "natural"; does not know pasture-raised from free-range; reads headline + do line only; needs words printed on the pack; must never feel dumb about what they usually buy.
- **Health-interested (tap layer).** Already reads labels; distrusts marketing words; wants the trap under the label and the reason it matters; hates hedging; wants a stance with a reason, not an authority name.
- Nobody reads the tap layer to get the top layer right.

## Broad items
A broad item (chicken, beef, eggs) names the parameters of the healthiest version, which hold for every cut. The card never recommends a cut or type. A specific search (breast, thighs, wings) lands on the same card with one line: the same marks apply. Separate cards only where the shopping really differs (ground vs steak, rotisserie).

## Tests every card passes
1. **5-second test.** Headline + do line, and the right pack is in the cart.
2. **Match test.** Every mark is a string printed on the pack.
3. **Why test.** One plain sentence on what it means for the shopper or the animal. No source name, no citation, no regulation number.
4. **No baseline talk.** Name the best and what to look for. Banned: "step up", "optional", "organic is", "either is fine" hedges.
5. **Trap test.** The tap layer names the label that sounds good and is not.
6. **One job per field.** Decision/why/take carry sourcing. Cooking, storage and regulation text do not live there.

## Warm means
Someone who has stood in that aisle many times and tells it plainly. No enthusiasm, no scolding, no fear, no "I", no price, no named brands.
