# fam-dairy — T2 family findings — 2026-10-23

Scope: F18–F24 and F37; 23 member cards. Read FAMILY-BRIEF.md first, FAMILIES.md, groups.json, the relevant dairy/pantryb/pantryc round-1 and round-2 findings, the 2026-10-22 decisions and current member KB entries. Findings for a separate copy builder; no KB changes.

**Evidence convention.** Best/close/floor/skip are the brief's editorial preferences, not demonstrated health rankings. Organic remains the floor; “100% grass-fed” is the required upper-rung dairy wording. Source references identify the factual ingredient/process evidence and traditional preferences; product examples do not establish that the complete preferred rung is stocked locally. URLs were located through this session's searches and supplied research, then opened; unsuccessful opens are explicitly marked. No producer names are recommendations.

**Sourcing overlay for every family.** Within the applicable recipe/process rung, look for a small local farm or maker identified on the pack, the agricultural source, and seasonal or batch information where relevant. Skip the large-scale commercial pattern—year-round long-distance supply and heavily branded packs without usable farm/source detail—in favor of the qualifying local option. This is the locked sourcing policy, not an inference that appearance or distance proves a health outcome. Farm-linked production and local organic vegetable sourcing are real models; neither establishes local availability for every product. [19a] [37a]

### F18 Fluid animal milk

F19 supplies the pasteurized-milk live-kefir alternative. Fermentation does not replace the raw-milk evaluation; no guaranteed alcohol-free claim. [18b] [19e] [37e]

### AISLE BRIEFING

1. Choose the food/use first: fluid milk F18, cultured dairy F19, cheese F20, dairy fats F21, plant drinks F22, cooking coconut milk F23, dessert F24 or live ferments F37. Processing differs within these routes. [18c] [21b] [22c] [23b]
2. Apply organic as the floor and explicit 100%-grass-fed dairy sourcing at the upper rungs; prefer a small local producer with usable source detail. Organic, raw, cream-top and a regional name answer different questions. [18b] [18c] [20b]
3. Read the actual formula and heat/culture statements; use the global ingredient screen with its sugar, culture, enzyme and recipe exceptions. [19b] [19c] [24a] [37c]
4. Under the locked avoidance-of-dairy route, simple nut drinks or whole coconut precede oat/soy; that is editorial routing, not proof of nutritional equivalence. Cooking coconut milk and beverage cartons are separate uses. [22a] [22b] [22c] [23b]
5. Check the relevant cold/frozen/dry-storage instructions; raw-dairy evaluation remains a separate decision. Ghee and unopened cooking-milk cans cannot inherit a universal chilled-case rule. [18b] [21d] [23b] [24c]

- **F18/F19/F37:** Raw milk, pasteurized milk fermented afterward, and a ferment heated afterward are different routes. F37's title should mean *no heat after fermentation*. If one undivided ladder is required, split it into vegetable ferments, brewed tea ferments and raw-milk kefir; retain the existing IDs until reassignment is approved. [18c] [19e] [37b]
- **F19:** The current Greek-first preference conflicts with the later source-based correction. A common family can rank source/full-fat/plain recipe, but cannot put Greek ahead of all other cultured foods or promise every member is raw/live. A complete human-food organic/100%-grass-fed/raw fresh-cheese retail rung remains unverified. [19c] [19d] [19e]
- **F20:** The literal “raw-milk cheese” title conflicts with its heated-cheese fallback. Proposed title: “Real cheese, raw milk preferred.” Thermized versus pasteurized ordering is not independently established; retain explicit process disclosure. Sixty days remains a jurisdictional review item, not a family safety statement. [20b] [20d]
- **F21:** Shared source/formula rule works; a shared raw-first or refrigerated-only rule fails for ghee. Split butter/cream from clarified fats only if the builder requires a single heat/storage ladder. [21b] [21d]
- **F22:** Almond, oat and soy cannot share the brief's complete relative preference ladder. Keep only the conditional formula screen at family level; otherwise split nut drinks, oat drinks and cooked soy drinks rather than let a delta reverse the ranking. [22a] [22b] [22c]
- **F23:** The two current member decisions converge on whole canned coconut milk. Round-2 dairy research admits a simple carton fallback while pantry research excludes it; a separate beverage route is needed before adopting that fallback. Glass-over-can superiority and universal can-lining health claims are not established. [23b] [P]
- **F24:** Added sugar is intentional, unlike F19/F22's plain purchases. Organic gum-free retail ice cream is evidenced; the complete 100%-grass-fed retail target is not established by that example. [24a]
- **Source refresh:** The plant-drink FAQ now gives ambient unopened storage for a specified bottle size; its older chilled-only description must not be propagated.
