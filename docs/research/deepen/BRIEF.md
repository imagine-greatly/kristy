# Deepen pass brief (Devon 2026-10-23)

Goal: every card becomes the most valuable, executable answer to "what is the healthiest, most natural, closest-to-source version of this food in a grocery store, and how do I find it." Findings only; a separate builder writes card copy.

## Stance (locked, do not re-derive)
Nature first, closest to source; organic a floor; per-item ladder + avoid list; small over big, unnamed; holistic sources judge, government = raw label meaning only; "look for". See docs/research/completeness/DECISIONS-2026-10-22.md (milk dialed; unsprayed hydroponic/greenhouse lettuce is a pick; no brand picks, no negative brand claims; no price; no treat/cure/prevent claims; zero first person).

## Sources
Holistic, traditional, niche only: ranchers, farmers, craft/traditional producers, WAPF-type, health-first writers, ingredient/process explainers. NO government, NO mainstream health sites, NO certifier "third-party testing" framing. Fetch every cited URL (scrapling or web fetch); record URL + 1-line what it supports. Never cite from memory. Archive fetched text under archive/deepen/<group>/.

## Per card, answer
1. Ladder: best, close second, floor, avoid; the exact label/shelf words that mark each rung.
2. Every shopper decision at this item (form, cut, grade, packaging, freshness, season, storage, where in store).
3. Niche concerns a health-first shopper has (processing, additives, feed/soil/spray, rancidity, packaging) with the holistic source.
4. Executable look-for/skip lines and one "do this at the counter/shelf" step.
5. What the current card gets wrong, is missing, or states thinly (read its decision/why/cart_pick/sources first; dialed + sourced = say COMPLETE, change nothing).
6. Aliases/questions shoppers actually ask that the card does not answer.

## Output
docs/research/deepen/<group>.md: per card id, ≤12 lines: GAPS, FINDINGS (each with URL), PROPOSED LADDER. Cards judged complete: one line. Do not edit the KB.
