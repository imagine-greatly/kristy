# Meat — T2 deepen findings

Research date: 2026-10-23. All linked pages were opened this run; each FINDINGS line records the URL and its support. Ladders are editorial applications of the locked nature-first stance, not experimentally established health rankings. Organic remains the floor for farmed foods; pasture, organic feed, and an identifiable small farm are separate questions. Producer pages establish methods or ingredient examples, not brand recommendations. Storage steps below supplement the package instructions; freshness, color, smell, or a farm story cannot establish microbiological safety.

## beef_grassfed_vs_grainfed
GAPS: Keep the 100% grass-fed bar; add organic pasture management, an actionable second rung, and frozen availability. Remove the sweeping claim that grass-fed labeling is wholly unregulated; the card conflates a withdrawn marketing standard with all label oversight.
FINDINGS: [Ranch explanation](https://www.alderspring.com/) — explicitly distinguishes lifetime grass/forage finishing from grain finishing and explains that grass-fed does not itself guarantee chemical-free management; ask about both finish and pasture treatments.
FINDINGS: [Ranch packaging FAQ](https://www.alderspring.com/faqs/packaging/) — describes meal-sized vacuum packs and flash freezing; frozen grass-finished cuts belong in the search alongside the fresh case, with intact seals protected from punctures.
FINDINGS: Counter step: ask “Which farm, any grain after weaning, and are the pasture and winter forage organic?”; request the producer's written answer when the counter cannot explain the finish ([ranch explanation](https://www.alderspring.com/)).
PROPOSED LADDER: BEST—organic, “100% grass-fed”/“grass-fed and grass-finished,” named grazing farm; CLOSE SECOND—organic with those same finish words, less farm detail, including frozen; FLOOR—plain organic grain-finished beef, explicitly lower; AVOID—grain-finished or unqualified “grass-fed” presented as equivalent, pasture artwork, flavored/solution-added meat.
GAPS—QUESTIONS: “Does grass-fed mean organic?”, “Is frozen grass-finished better than fresh grain-finished?”, “Does hay count?”, “What did the animal eat in winter?”

GAPS—QUESTIONS: “Steak versus pot roast?”, “Chuck roast versus pot roast?”, “Which cut for a quick pan meal?”, “Does a higher grade override grass finishing?”
