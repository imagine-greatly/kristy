FINDINGS: [Traditional preparation discussion](https://www.westonaprice.org/health-topics/why-we-cook-and-chop-ferment-salt-and-dry/) supports soaking legumes and thorough cooking as a traditional preference; the [grain FAQ’s seven-hour answer](https://www.westonaprice.org/health-topics/grains-seeds-nuts-beans-faqs/) specifically answers a brown-rice question, not a pea/lentil protocol.
FINDINGS: [Grower-linked whole-lentil instructions](https://hodmedods.co.uk/products/speckled-green-lentils) and [organic split-pea instructions](https://hodmedods.co.uk/products/split-green-peas) distinguish optional soaking from cooking necessity and describe crop/water-dependent texture; [variety discussion](https://hodmedods.co.uk/blogs/news/first-british-lentil-harvest-underway) identifies whole, shape-retaining lentils. Neither supports a universal acidic seven-hour rule.
PROPOSED LADDER: BEST fresh-crop organic dried pulses from a grower disclosing field/spray practices, traditionally prepared at home; CLOSE SECOND organic dried pulses with origin and whole/split form stated; FLOOR plain organic cooked pulses with no sauce or flavor system; AVOID conventional pulses, stale/damp stock and instant flavored soup blends.
LOOK FOR / SKIP: Whole green/French-style lentils suit shape-retaining dishes; split peas and split red lentils suit softer soups/purees. Neither color is a universal health grade; “French-style” is not by itself proof of French origin.
SHELF STEP: Match the form to soup versus salad, read origin/ingredients, choose a sealed dry pack, sort/rinse and cook until tender using its directions; keep any traditional soak separate from a rigid all-pulse rule and store dry stock airtight.

## applesauce
GAPS: Organic apples alone is already a strong pick, but it needs the grocery form/packaging ladder and a workable apples-plus-water option. Replace vague annual-residue rhetoric with stable ingredient/sourcing criteria; no new ranking-year assertion is needed.
FINDINGS: [Farm’s one-ingredient example](https://dutchmeadowsfarm.com/store/product/organic-apple-sauce) lists organic apples alone in glass; [small-batch farm example](https://allheartsfarm.com/product/nyc-organic-local-applesauce/) uses organic apples and water. Water need not disqualify an otherwise simple sauce.
PROPOSED LADDER: BEST gently cooked seasonal organic apples at home or a small maker’s fresh equivalent; CLOSE SECOND small-batch organic apples-only sauce in glass; FLOOR organic unsweetened applesauce with apples/water and no added sweetener or flavoring; AVOID sugar, corn syrup, juice-concentrate sweetening and flavored dessert blends. An apples-only jar remains the simplest shelf target.
LOOK FOR / SKIP: Look for “organic” and inspect every ingredient even when the front says “no added sugar”; named cinnamon is different from an unspecified flavor. Prefer glass as a packaging choice without inventing pouch-contamination claims.
SHELF STEP: In jarred fruit or the farm-food section, choose the smallest jar finished within its after-opening directions; inspect the seal and refrigerate after opening. Smooth/chunky is a texture choice, not a sourcing grade.

## salad_dressing
GAPS: The oil/short-list rule is strong but misses EVOO/seed-oil blends and real cultured-dairy ranch/Caesar options. The cited work is an ingredient review; calling it testing overstates the evidence.
FINDINGS: [Ingredient review](https://mamavation.com/food/salad-dressing-marinade.html) supports checking oils, preservatives, flavors and sweeteners across the full list; refrigeration alone does not establish that a dressing qualifies.
FINDINGS: [Traditional ranch method](https://nourishedkitchen.com/kefir-ranch-dressing/) shows that kefir/buttermilk-style dairy, named herbs and mayonnaise can make a simple creamy dressing; oil screening must include the mayonnaise sublist.
PROPOSED LADDER: BEST organic EVOO with lemon/raw vinegar and herbs mixed at the table; CLOSE SECOND a fresh organic cultured-dairy dressing with fully disclosed acceptable-fat mayonnaise; FLOOR organic bottled vinaigrette/dressing whose only added oil is EVOO and whose ingredients are fully named; AVOID soybean/canola blends, HFCS, EDTA, gums and undisclosed flavors. Pure cold-pressed avocado oil remains the locked conditional alternative.
LOOK FOR / SKIP: Look for actual extra virgin olive oil as the sole added oil; “made with olive oil,” “light,” “keto” and “refrigerated” do not settle the recipe. Whole-milk cultured dairy can be a real-food creamy base.
SHELF STEP: Read the oils and any mayonnaise sublist before comparing flavors; choose a size used within its opened-storage directions, refrigerate as directed, and make only a small batch of fresh dairy dressing.

## bbq_sauce_marinades
GAPS: “Natural sweetener” does not establish a good sauce when sweetener still leads; barbecue finishing sauces and marinades have different bases. Remove the categorical implication that unspecified natural flavor necessarily contains MSG.
FINDINGS: [Condiment ingredient review](https://mamavation.com/food/favorite-sauce-stack-condiments-bad-best.html) flags HFCS, seed oils, added smoke flavor and preservatives; use its label concerns without importing brand judgments or disease claims.
FINDINGS: [Fermented barbecue example](https://culturesforhealth.com/blogs/recipes/kombucha-recipe-kombucha-fermented-bbq-sauce) demonstrates fermentation with a sour culture and tomato/spices; fermentation and sweetener choice remain separate checks, since that recipe also includes brown sugar.
PROPOSED LADDER: BEST homemade organic vinegar/mustard/herb marinade or a genuinely fermented simple sauce; CLOSE SECOND an organic tomato/vinegar/spice barbecue sauce with little or no sweetener; FLOOR short-list organic sauce using modest molasses/honey/maple, acceptable oil if any, and named seasonings; AVOID corn syrup, seed oils, smoke flavor, MSG/yeast extract and preservative-heavy blends.
LOOK FOR / SKIP: Look for vinegar, tomato, mustard, garlic and spices before sweetener; for teriyaki, choose naturally brewed/fermented tamari rather than hydrolyzed protein. Named smoked paprika is different from liquid smoke flavoring; “sugar-free” does not settle sweeteners or additives.
SHELF STEP: Decide marinade versus finishing sauce, compare the leading ingredients, and inspect all oils/sweeteners; refrigerate after opening as directed. A fermented ingredient on a shelf-stable bottle does not promise live cultures in the finished sauce.

## pesto
GAPS: The existing oil rule is good, but the simplified classic recipe omits garlic, salt and sheep’s cheese; nut substitutions are variations, not the exact Genovese formula. Missing fresh-versus-heat-treated comparison and a product-specific opened-use direction.
FINDINGS: [Traditional mortar-pesto recipe](https://www.pestochampionship.it/en/mortar-made-pesto-sauce-recipe/) specifies basil, pine nuts, garlic, Parmesan, Fiore Sardo, salt and EVOO; supports a precise traditional ingredient target without requiring protected-name certification as the shopping answer.
FINDINGS: [Traditional-food pesto method](https://nourishedkitchen.com/garlic-scape-pesto/) uses fresh herbs, EVOO, nuts and cheese, permits seasonal variations, and supports freezing; a seasonal scape pesto can qualify without being classic Genovese.
PROPOSED LADDER: BEST freshly crushed organic seasonal basil/herbs with EVOO, fresh nuts and real aged cheese from a small maker or home kitchen; CLOSE SECOND a refrigerated organic pesto with the same fully disclosed recipe; FLOOR an organic shelf-stable pesto with EVOO as its only added oil and no unnecessary fillers; AVOID seed-oil blends, starch/thickener-heavy sauces, flavorings and cheese substitutes.
LOOK FOR / SKIP: Look for organic herbs, EVOO alone and explicitly named cheese/nuts; basil pictures and “Italian-style” do not establish the oil. Walnut, seed or dairy-free pesto can be an honest variation when ingredients are fully disclosed.
SHELF STEP: Compare the refrigerated and jarred ingredient lists, ask whether a fresh tub was heated, and choose a small sealed portion; follow its opened-use directions and freeze excess in portions instead of applying a universal one-week promise.
