## beef_grassfed_vs_grainfed
NEW FINDINGS — independent second for lifetime forage plus organic management: [another grazing farm](https://applecreekfarm.me/pages/how-we-farm) explicitly separates organic from non-organic batches, feeds organic hay/baleage in winter, and finishes without grain; corroborates [round-1 ranch](https://www.alderspring.com/). Hay is forage, not a grain finish.
NEW FINDINGS — regional/seasonal: the Maine farm targets autumn harvest after summer grazing; [Devon farm](https://www.farm2fork.co.uk/blogs/blog/grass-fed-beef-cuts-guide) also specifies conserved forage and no grain. Ask about the actual finishing season, not a universal “spring beef” rule.
NEW FINDINGS — independent second for frozen sourcing: [seasonal farm's freezing method](https://www.farm2fork.co.uk/pages/why-we-freeze-our-meat) describes fast-freezing cuts for year-round supply; retain intact meal packs and refrigerated thawing, independently described by [producer handling FAQ](https://grasslandbeef.com/pages/customer-service).
CORRECTIONS to round 1: [packaging FAQ](https://www.alderspring.com/faqs/packaging/) supports vacuum packing, not its attributed flash-freezing claim. No evidence establishes less farm detail or freezing as intrinsically inferior. Blanket legal statements about an unregulated claim remain outside this holistic evidence; do not reinstate them.
QUESTIONS — unanswered in current fields: Does winter hay or baleage count? Can an organic farm sell a non-organic beef batch? Is frozen autumn-finished beef preferable to a newly wrapped winter grain finish? Was the pasture sprayed even though the animal ate only forage?
LADDER CHANGES — BEST: look for “organic” + “100% grass-fed”/“grass-fed and grass-finished,” named farm and no-grain finishing account; SECOND: same organic finish standard with less husbandry detail; FLOOR: “organic” grain-finished, explicitly lower; AVOID: unqualified “grass-fed,” “regenerative” or pasture pictures used instead of finish/feed answers, and solution-added products.
LADDER CHANGES — counter step: request the farm and finishing ration, including winter forage; choose a meal-sized whole cut or plain grind, fresh or sealed frozen at the same rung.
