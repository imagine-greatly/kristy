## salmon_wild_vs_farmed
GAPS: Keep wild/frozen-wild first. Remove the unsupported country-wide antibiotic comparisons and “never swam anywhere” rhetoric; feed composition varies. Coho's name and flesh color cannot establish wild origin. Missing whole side versus portions, pin bones, seasonal fresh versus year-round frozen, packaging and thawing.
FINDINGS: [Wellness Mama, fish farming](https://wellnessmama.com/health/fish-farming/) — describes formulated feeds, variable omega-3 content and higher omega-6 in farmed salmon; also acknowledges farmed portions can contain more total omega-3 because they contain more fat. Supports a feed-based preference without claiming all farms have identical rations.
FINDINGS: [Fisher family's salmon collection](https://eatwildsalmon.com/collections/wild-alaskan-salmon) — documents summer Bristol Bay harvest, flash freezing, whole fillets and portion packs; supports asking the actual harvest season and selecting a form that avoids repeatedly thawing a whole side.
FINDINGS: [Producer's aquaculture description](https://cookeseafood.com/divisions/cooke-aquaculture/) — explicitly includes farmed coho; “coho,” “Pacific” or pink flesh alone is insufficient. Look for “wild-caught” plus a named harvest origin.
FINDINGS: [Fishermen's association, why frozen](https://www.alfafish.org/why-frozen) — rapid freezing preserves harvest quality and extends seasonal supply; a distant counter's never-frozen display does not automatically outrank promptly frozen wild fish.
FINDINGS: [Seafood producer's thawing explanation](https://sitkaseafoodmarket.com/blogs/culinary/from-the-test-kitchen/how-to-thaw-vacuum-packaged-fish) — vacuum packaging limits drying and fat oxidation; introduce air before overnight refrigerator thawing and follow package directions. Do not thaw on the counter.
PROPOSED LADDER: BEST traceable small-fisher “wild-caught” salmon, chilled promptly, fresh in its regional season or flash-frozen; CLOSE SECOND sealed frozen “wild-caught Alaska” portions, fish only; FLOOR canned wild salmon with salmon and optional salt; AVOID opaque farmed salmon, color as a sourcing test, flavored/glazed portions with unwanted oils or additives.
PROPOSED LADDER — counter/freezer action: Ask “Which species, where caught, when caught or frozen, and was this thawed for display?” Choose skin-on whole fillets for a shared meal or individually sealed portions for separate meals; ask whether pin bones remain. Aliases/questions: “keta/chum,” “pink versus sockeye,” “salmon side,” “skin-on,” “pin-bone out,” “is coho always wild?”, “fresh wild salmon out of season?”
