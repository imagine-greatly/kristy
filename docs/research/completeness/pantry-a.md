### RECOMMENDED CALL per card
- honey_adulteration (THIN): Raw honey, preferably unfiltered, from a named local producer; never heated. WAPF tiers it Best ("raw honey, preferably unfiltered"; not for babies) and lists "heated honey" under Avoid; WAPF FAQ: heating "does not become toxic but the enzymes may be compromised". Step: ask the seller if it was heated or filtered; crystallized is normal. Adulteration claim stays unsourced; keep it out. (wapf-shopping-guide-categories, wapf-faq-miscellaneous-food-questions)
- maple_syrup: 100% pure maple syrup, darker grades preferred (WAPF holds darker syrup carries the stronger flavor and minerals; Fancy/light grade was driven by candy makers). Imitation table syrups are mainly HFCS. Step: label reads only "maple syrup", pick Dark/Amber from a named sugarhouse. Maple sugar listed Best. (wapf-maple-sugar-gift-from-the-indians; guide)
- coffee_beans (CONFLICT): WAPF lists coffee under Avoid (Nourishing Traditions pp. 51-52 per its reply). If Devon's stance is "healthiest version if bought", Kresser (mold-illness page) holds conventional coffee is high-mold and names tested brands (Bulletproof, Kion): both are product-endorsement voices. Call: lead with the WAPF stance (not a staple), then if bought: organic whole bean, sealed, fresh-roasted, from a roaster that states it tests for mold. Admin decision needed on the stance.
- pick_juice: WAPF Avoid list includes "fruit and vegetable juices" and "concentrated fruit juices"; WAPF FAQ says Nourishing Traditions advises against juice, carrot juice with cream the exception. Call: whole fruit first; if juice, only fresh-pressed or raw, small, with a meal; never from concentrate. Fresh-pressed step is my inference, not WAPF text. (guide; faq)
- pick_canned_tomatoes: WAPF holds acidity makes can-lining BPA leach more and BPA-free linings hard for tomatoes; preferred are frozen, fermented or home-preserved. Guide Good tier: "canned organic tomato products without sugar and citric acid in BPA-free containers". Call: tomatoes in glass jars, or cartons, organic, ingredients tomatoes and salt, no citric acid or sugar. Kresser: "avoid canned foods" (linings contain BPA or a substitute). (wapf-making-the-most-of-autumn-abundance; guide; kresser-bpa)
- kimchi: Refrigerated, unpasteurized, ferment-only list (cabbage, chile, garlic, ginger, salt, fish sauce or salted shrimp for umami). Nourished Kitchen: traditional kimchi ferments warm then finishes cold. Step: buy from the cold case, lid may bulge, no vinegar, sugar or preservatives. Store-bought "unpasteurized" check is a general rule, not in the fetched page. (nourishedkitchen-kimchi-recipe; wapf-faq-fermented-foods)
- kombucha: WAPF holds plain over flavored, and shelf-stable store kombucha is fermented for less time. Guide Best: organic kombucha from organic black tea in glass. Call: plain, refrigerated, raw, glass bottle; check sugar; skip juice-flavored. (wapf-kombucha, guide)
- bagels, buns_rolls, pastries_muffins, pick_english_muffins, pick_pita_naan (THIN, one rule): WAPF tiers: Best = sourdough/soaked/sprouted breads from freshly ground organic whole grain with no added gluten, soy, bran, hydrogenated oil; Avoid = breads with ascorbic acid, brown rice syrup, sugar. Muffins/cookies Best = organic natural sweeteners, soaked or sprouted flour, butter or coconut oil. WAPF sourdough bagel recipe uses starter, flour, coconut oil; WAPF bakery list names a pita maker (Columbia County Bread & Granola) and sourdough English muffins (Gluten-Free Sourdough Co.). Call: sourdough or sprouted version, flour first, no added gluten or sugar; pastry only if butter-based and sweetener natural. (guide; wapf-sg2021-page54; wapf-sourdough-bagels)
- broth: Bone broth from pastured, non-GMO-fed animals, ideally homemade; canned broth only if without additives (add gelatin). Avoid aseptic-box stock, bouillon cubes, dehydrated soups with MSG. Step: look for "bones" first, short list, and a gel when chilled (gel is WAPF-stocks teaching). (wapf-stocks; guide)
- flour_basics, grains_beyond_rice, pasta_dry, breakfast_cereal, specialty_flours (soak/sprout): WAPF holds grains and flour should be soaked or sprouted; Best flour = freshly ground then soaked, or organic sprouted; Avoid = unsoaked whole grain flour, bleached white, extruded cold cereal, unsoaked granola, white-flour pasta, quick-cooking grains. Pasta: all pasta is "Good" tier but organic whole grain, buckwheat or corn pasta; quinoa and bulgur Avoid. Step: buy sprouted flour or a small mill's fresh-ground, soak overnight with a little acid; cereal: soaked oats cooked, not boxed. WAPF phytic-acid and sprouting files back this. (guide; wapf-phytic-acid; wapf-sprouting; wapf-be-kind-to-grains; nourishedkitchen-grainsqa)
- cooking_oils: WAPF Best is animal fat (lard, tallow, ghee), butter, coconut and cold olive oil; Avoid industrial liquid vegetable oils and cottonseed. Note the guide places avocado oil in Avoid for fried snacks. Call: tallow, lard, butter/ghee or coconut for cooking, extra-virgin olive oil cool; pasture-raised fat via named sellers (SG2021 lists many lard/tallow sellers). Card should keep avocado out. (wapf-the-skinny-on-fats; guide; wapf-sg2021-page16)
- pork_cuts_and_enhanced: Pasture or woodland pork, no added solution, no nitrite-celery shortcuts for cured cuts. Lard-podcast producer holds roughly 98 percent of US pork is raised indoors and that pork fat reflects diet. Step: buy from a farm that says pasture/woodland and soy-free feed; WAPF chapters list such farms by region. Remove the "hormones banned, freebie" line (reassurance). (wapf-lard-podcast; wapf-chapter-pittsburgh-pastured-woodland-pork; guide)
- white_fish (THIN): Wild-caught named species, firm, mild smell; farmed (tilapia/catfish/trout/salmon) is on WAPF Avoid. Wellness Mama says wild-caught white fish such as cod, mahi mahi or halibut for fish dishes. Cod liver and liver oil are WAPF territory but not a counter pick. Call: wild cod or halibut by name, fresh or frozen-at-sea. (guide; wellnessmama-fish-farming; wellnessmama-fish-sticks)
- lobster (THIN): Live, active, or hard-frozen tail; WAPF (Nagel) holds lobster and crawfish are rich in the same fat-soluble vitamins as crab, concentrated in tomalley and fatty organs. Step: ask for the tomalley kept; eat it only when the water is clean. Disease wording not used. (wapf-crab-high-vitamin)
- pick_fish_sticks (THIN): Best is homemade from wild white fish with egg and coconut-flour breading fried in coconut oil or butter (Wellness Mama; WAPF fish cakes baked variant). For store-bought, the guide Avoid tier hits seafood with soy or vegetable oil, hydrolyzed protein, citric acid, farmed fish. Call: whole-fillet wild fish sticks only if breading and frying oil list has no seed oil, soy or hydrolyzed protein; otherwise make them. (wellnessmama-fish-sticks; wapf-fish-cakes; guide)
