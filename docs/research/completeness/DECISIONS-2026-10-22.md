# Completeness pass: stance calls (2026-10-22)

Made under the standing brief: guide the shopper to the healthiest version of every item, holistic first, as close to nature as possible, organic a floor. Holistic and traditional sources judge; government is raw label meaning only.

| Item | Call |
| --- | --- |
| Tofu | Rank below fermented soy (tempeh, miso, natto, tamari) from organic beans. Tofu stays a card, organic only, never the lead soy pick. |
| Plant milks, plant butter | Honest ladder: dairy first (raw, then 100% grass-fed). Plant milk is a fallback for someone who avoids dairy: shortest ingredient list, no gums or added oils, nuts/coconut over oat/soy. Canned coconut milk over aseptic carton. |
| Cocoa | Real cocoa or cacao, short list, organic; no "Dutched with alkali" or sugar-first mixes. Treat as an occasional item, not a staple. |
| Tea | Organic loose-leaf or whole-leaf bags; herbal from named herbs; skip flavored with "natural flavors". |
| Snack bars | Lead with the whole-food alternative (nuts, fruit, jerky, cheese). If a bar is chosen: nuts, dates, nothing else. Granola and energy bars are the low rung. |
| Ketchup, mustard | Fermented or unsweetened, short list, vinegar not corn syrup. |
| Coconut water | Fresh or cold, nothing added, over high-pressure or from-concentrate. |
| Avocado oil | Fine when the maker shows it is pure and cold-pressed; otherwise olive oil, tallow, butter, ghee, coconut lead. Never say purity cannot be proven as the whole answer. |
| Canned soup | Objection is MSG, yeast extract and seed oils as much as sodium. Pick: short list, bone broth base. Better: make it. |
| Grass-fed | "100% grass-fed" stays the bar for milk and beef. Grain-finished or "grass-fed" without 100% drops a rung. |
| Hydroponic / greenhouse lettuce | Unsprayed, living (roots on) hydroponic or greenhouse lettuce is a recommended pick, alongside local soil-grown. |
| Reassurance lines | "Safe levels", "buy conventional", "low-residue", "just as good", "broadly comparable" are rewritten to the nature-first ladder, claim-locked, no new claims. |
| Government sourcing | Replaced by holistic sources where one exists; kept only for the raw meaning of a label term. |
| Coffee | Occasional, not a staple. If bought: organic whole bean from a roaster that states it tests for mold. No detox or can-lining health wording. |
| Brand picks | No brand picks and no negative brand claims; method and label picks only. |
