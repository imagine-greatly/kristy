## Confirmed from primary text

### A Greener World: Animal Welfare Approved (AWA)
- Audited: YES. "AGW's independent trained auditors visit every farm in the program at least once a year to verify they're meeting the standards."
- Guarantees: animals outdoors on pasture or range for their entire lives, audited production, transport and slaughter.
- Quote: "the only label that guarantees animals are raised outdoors on pasture or range for their entire lives on an independent farm"
- URL fetched: https://agreenerworld.org/certifications/animal-welfare-approved/

### A Greener World: Certified Grassfed
- Audited: YES (same AGW audit text, AWA page). The Certified Grassfed page itself returned only a fee page.
- Guarantees: all-grass-and-forage diet on pasture or range plus welfare management.
- Quote: "Guarantees 100 percent grass and forage diet on pasture or range, and high-welfare management."
- URL fetched: https://agreenerworld.org/certifications/certified-grassfed/ (quote is the site-wide tagline on that page; no standard text there)

### A Greener World: Certified Regenerative
- Audited: not stated on the page fetched (AGW-wide audit line is on the AWA page only).
- Guarantees: a regenerative certification covering soil, ecosystems, water/air, workers and animals; the page gives no numeric thresholds.
- Quote: "Covering all aspects of regenerative agriculture, Certified Regenerative by AGW meets you where you are as your partner on a regenerative journey."
- URL fetched: https://agreenerworld.org/certifications/certified-regenerative/

### Certified Humane (HFAC): Pasture Raised, laying hens
- Audited: YES. "Certification indicates that a farm or producer has been evaluated and found to be in compliance with our standards at the time of audit or inspection." (https://certifiedhumane.org/how-we-work/our-standards/)
- Guarantees: hens on pasture 12 months of the year, mainly living vegetation, indoors at night only; 2.5 acres per 1000 birds.
- Quotes (Standard_LayingHens-2023.pdf, text extracted from PDF streams, line breaks in source):
  - "Pasture Raised is a management system where adult birds are kept on pasture 12 months of the year, in an outside area that is mainly covered with living vegetation."
  - "The minimum outdoor space requirement is 2.5 acres (1 hectare) per 1000 birds to meet the Animal Care Standards for Pasture Raised."
  - "Free Range: 2 square feet per bird, weather permitting outdoor access" (housing appendix, same PDF)
- 2.5 acres / 1000 birds works out to about 109 sq ft per hen (arithmetic, not a quote).
- Note: the "Free Range vs Pasture Raised" article URL returned a 404 revision page; the standards PDF is the primary source used.
- URL fetched: https://certifiedhumane.org/wp-content/uploads/Standard_LayingHens-2023.pdf
- Not fetched: Certified Humane pasture-raised acres for broiler chickens or pigs (chicken/pig PDFs were not read).

### American Grassfed Association (AGA)
- Audited: YES. "...pass regular third-party, on-farm inspections to remain" certified. (https://www.americangrassfed.org/)
- Guarantees: grass-and-forage-only diet, no confinement, no antibiotics or added hormones.
- Quotes: "Animals are fed only grass and forage from weaning until harvest." / "Animals are raised on pasture without confinement." / "Animals are never treated with antibiotics or added growth hormones."
- URL fetched: https://www.americangrassfed.org/about-us/our-standards/ and https://www.americangrassfed.org/ (the third-party inspection line is on the home page).

### Real Organic Project
- Audited: NOT STATED in fetched text (no inspection wording found on the pages fetched).
- Guarantees: whole-farm, soil-grown, pasture-based; no hydroponics, no CAFOs.
- Quotes: "farmer-led, soil-grown, pasture-raised WHOLE farm certification with labor protections" / "Farms cannot have any hydroponic or Concentrated Animal Feeding Operation (CAFO) production." / "For ruminants, at least 40% of diet must come from pasture during grazing season." / "The standards forbid routine preventative antibiotics and many physical alterations like tail docking and de-beaking."
- URLs fetched: https://realorganicproject.org/ and https://realorganicproject.org/get-certified/standards/

### Marine Stewardship Council (MSC)
- Audited: YES. "Fisheries are assessed against three core principles by accredited independent certifiers."
- Guarantees: wild fishery assessed against the MSC Fisheries Standard; blue label on the catch.
- Quotes: "When a fishery is certified, its catch can be sold with the blue MSC label." / "that meet the MSC Standard are independently certified"
- URLs fetched: https://www.msc.org/standards-and-certification/fisheries-standard and https://www.msc.org/en-us/what-we-are-doing/our-approach Chain of Custody standard is only mentioned as a link, not read.

### Aquaculture Stewardship Council (ASC)
- Audited: YES. "all independently verified across the supply chain"
- Guarantees: farmed seafood to science-based requirements with independent verification.
- Quote: "Science-based standards. Independent certification. Verifiable and measurable environmental and social improvement across seafood farming and supply chains."
- URL fetched: https://asc-aqua.org/

### Best Aquaculture Practices (BAP, GSA)
- Audited: YES (third-party, by its own description).
- Guarantees: certification across the production chain; the page lists standards modules (social, animal welfare, environmental).
- Quote: "the only third-party aquaculture certification program that encompasses the entire production chain, including the processing plant, farm, hatchery and feed mill."
- URL fetched: https://www.bapcertification.org/ "Only" is BAP's own superlative; do not repeat it in card copy.

### Land to Market Verified / Ecological Outcome Verification (Savory Institute)
- Audited: PARTLY. Outcomes are monitored and soil is lab-tested; the page does not use the word "third-party".
- Guarantees: product from land whose monitoring data show regeneration.
- Quotes: "If EOV data indicates land regeneration, products derived from that landbase may apply to carry the coveted Land to Market Verified seal." / "The Land to Market Verified seal gives consumers confidence that products came from land that is verified regenerative and backed by scientific data."
- URLs fetched: https://savory.global/land-to-market/ and https://savory.global/eov/

## USDA terms (law.cornell.edu, e-CFR)

### 7 CFR 205.239 (organic livestock living conditions)
- Gov-regulated. Quotes: "management on pasture and daily grazing throughout the grazing season(s) to meet the requirements of § 205.237" and "Ruminant slaughter stock ... shall be maintained on pasture for each day that the finishing period corresponds with the grazing season for the geographical location. Yards, feeding pads, or feedlots may be used to provide finish feeding rations."
- Related, 7 CFR 205.237: ruminants "provided with an average of not less than 30 percent of their dry matter intake from grazing" (fetched at https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/7/205.237).
- URL fetched: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/7/205.239
