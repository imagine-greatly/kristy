Shop mode · restructure

The current shop screen, one page per group

Same screen, same colors, same bottom bar. What changes: the single scroll becomes pages. The aisle name drops back to a small label, and the guidance is the headline. Swipe, tap Next, or use arrow keys. Checkboxes work. Every card's text is verbatim from the corpus (aisle entries, item cards, family tips); nothing is written for the deck. Long cards scroll inside the card.

Read top to bottom, in the order a shopper acts

Name. Items to tick. Grab: the one decision. At the shelf: numbered checks. What the label means. Leave it. Cannot find it. At home. Why. Then the illustration at the bottom.

Same parts as today

Top bar with position, segments, gold hairline, cards with the gold top edge, checkbox rows, "Next: ..." row, Scan / Ask / + bar. Nothing new to learn.

Checkboxes stay where they are

They live on the page for the items it covers, as they do now. Last time I moved them to the list. That was wrong for a restructure.
