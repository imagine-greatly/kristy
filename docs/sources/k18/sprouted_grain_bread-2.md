**Sprouted bread** is a type of [bread](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bread "Bread") made from [whole grains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_grain "Whole grain") that have been allowed to [sprout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sprouting "Sprouting") ([germinate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germination "Germination")) before being milled into flour.

Although sprouted breads like Essene bread (named after the [Essenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essenes "Essenes"))[[1]](#cite_note-j129-1) and Ezekiel bread (named after an ancient bread recipe found in the Bible in [Ezekiel 4:9](https://bible.oremus.org/?passage=Ezekiel%204:9&version=nrsv))) contain few ingredients other than milled sprouted grain and water, some sprouted breads are made with added [flour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flour "Flour") or [gluten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gluten "Gluten").[[2]](#cite_note-h152-2)

Overview
--------

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Sprouted_bread&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Overview")]

[![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/47/Sprouted_Wild_Yeasted_Whole_Wheat_Bread_%284656526411%29.jpg/250px-Sprouted_Wild_Yeasted_Whole_Wheat_Bread_%284656526411%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sprouted_Wild_Yeasted_Whole_Wheat_Bread_(4656526411).jpg)

Sprouted wild-yeasted [whole wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_wheat "Whole wheat") bread.

Sprouted breads contain the whole, sprouted grain (the [kernel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed "Seed"), or berry) of various seeds. They are different from [white bread](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_bread "White bread") inasmuch as white breads are made from ground wheat [endosperm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endosperm "Endosperm") (after removal of the [bran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bran "Bran") and [germ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cereal_germ "Cereal germ")). Whole grain breads include the bran, germ, and endosperm, therefore providing more naturally occurring [fiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dietary_fiber "Dietary fiber"), [vitamins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitamin "Vitamin") and [proteins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein "Protein"). Sprouted (or [germinated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germinated "Germinated")) grain breads have roughly the same amount of vitamins per gram, and 47% less gluten than regular bread.[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

A comparison of nutritional analyses shows that sprouted grains contain about 75% of the carbohydrates, slightly higher protein and about 40% of the fat when compared to whole grains.[[3]](#cite_note-3)[[4]](#cite_note-4)

In addition to wheat, sprouted breads may contain grains and legumes, such as [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet "Millet"), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley "Barley"), [oat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oat "Oat"), [lentil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lentil "Lentil") and [soy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soy "Soy"). Bread that is made from an array of grains and legumes can provide a complete set of [amino acids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amino_acid "Amino acid"), the building blocks of proteins. Sprouted breads may contain slightly more trace minerals and nutrients than non-sprouted breads. Other than that, they supply much the same advantages as whole grain breads over refined grain breads, such as lowered risk of coronary heart disease.[[5]](#cite_note-5)

Essene bread
------------

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Sprouted_bread&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Essene bread")]

[![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/26/Essene_Bread_70pct_Rye_Sproud_30pct_Spelt_cut.JPG/250px-Essene_Bread_70pct_Rye_Sproud_30pct_Spelt_cut.JPG?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Essene_Bread_70pct_Rye_Sproud_30pct_Spelt_cut.JPG)

A loaf of Essene bread made from 70% [sprouted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sprouting "Sprouting") [rye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rye "Rye") and 30% [whole-grain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whole_grain "Whole grain") [spelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spelt "Spelt").

Essene bread is a simple form of sprouted grain bread made from sprouted wheat and prepared at a low temperature. Proponents of [raw foods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_foodism "Raw foodism") often eat it uncooked or slightly heated.[[6]](#cite_note-6)[[7]](#cite_note-7) The [Essenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essenes "Essenes"), an [ascetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asceticism_in_Judaism "Asceticism in Judaism") [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism "Judaism") sect that flourished from the [second century BC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_century_BC "2nd century BC") through the [first century AD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_century "1st century"), are credited with the technique and basic recipe for Essene bread[[8]](#cite_note-livestrong1-8) despite a dearth of scholarly evidence for the claim. Sprouting and low-temperature preparation ensures the maximum possible [vitamin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitamin "Vitamin") content for the [foodstuff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food "Food").[[8]](#cite_note-livestrong1-8) Sprouting also breaks down the [lectins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lectin "Lectin") and other substances that some individuals may be [sensitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_intolerance "Food intolerance") or [allergic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_allergy "Food allergy") to.[[8]](#cite_note-livestrong1-8)
