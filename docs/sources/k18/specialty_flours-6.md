Almond meal being made by hand

**Almond meal**, **almond flour** or **ground almond** is made from [ground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mill_(grinding) "Mill (grinding)") sweet [almonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almond "Almond"). Almond flour is usually made with blanched almonds (no skin), whereas almond meal can be made with whole or blanched almonds. The consistency is more like [corn meal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corn_meal "Corn meal") than [wheat flour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat_flour "Wheat flour").[[1]](#cite_note-masterclass-1)

It is used in [pastry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastry "Pastry") and confectionery – in the manufacture of almond [macarons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaron "Macaron") and [macaroons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaroon "Macaroon") and other sweet pastries, in [cake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cake "Cake") and [pie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pie "Pie") filling, such as Austrian *[Sachertorte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachertorte "Sachertorte")* – and is one of the two main ingredients of [marzipan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marzipan "Marzipan") and [almond paste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almond_paste "Almond paste"). In France, almond meal is an important ingredient in *[frangipane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frangipane "Frangipane")*, the filling of traditional *[galette des Rois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galette_des_Rois "Galette des Rois")* cake.

Almond meal has recently become important in baking items for those on [low-carbohydrate diets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-carbohydrate_diet "Low-carbohydrate diet"). It adds moistness and a rich nutty taste to baked goods. Items baked with almond meal tend to be calorie-dense.

Almonds have high levels of [polyunsaturated fats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyunsaturated_fats "Polyunsaturated fats"). Typically, the [omega 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega_6 "Omega 6") [fatty acids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatty_acid "Fatty acid") in almonds are protected from [oxidation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxidation "Oxidation") by the surface skin and [vitamin E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitamin_E "Vitamin E"). When almonds are ground, this protective skin is broken and exposed surface area increases dramatically, greatly enhancing the nut's tendency to oxidize.[[2]](#cite_note-2)[[3]](#cite_note-3)
