How are naan and pita bread different?
--------------------------------------

* Naan is richer in manganese, phosphorus, fiber, and magnesium, while pita bread is higher in vitamin B1, selenium, folate, vitamin B2, and iron.
* Naan covers your daily need for manganese 40% more than pita bread.
* Naan contains 18 times more saturated fat than pita bread. Naan contains 2.907g of saturated fat, while pita bread contains 0.166g.

[Bread, naan, whole wheat, commercially prepared, refrigerated](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-app.html#/food-details/174077/nutrients) and [Bread, pita, white, enriched](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-app.html#/food-details/174915/nutrients) types were used in this article.
