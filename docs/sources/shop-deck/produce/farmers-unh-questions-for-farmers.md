Want to get to know your local Farmer? Here is a way to get to know your farmers by asking them a few simple questions- just print off the [10 Questions for Farmers](https://extension.unh.edu/sites/default/files/migrated_unmanaged_files/Resource006107_Rep8632.pdf "10 Questions for Farmers") sheet.

10 Questions to Ask the Farmers

1.   How do you recommend storing this fruit/vegetable?
2.   What is your favorite way to eat this fruit/vegetable?
3.   What kind of flavor does this fruit/vegetable have?
4.   Do you have any tips on how to get kids to eat this fruit/vegetable?
5.   What will be coming into season soon?
6.   Where is your farm? How long has it been in operation?
7.   Could you explain you’re farming practices?
8.   What is the best way to get the most out of this fruit/vegetable? (i.e. can all parts be eaten?)
9.   How long will this fruit/vegetable last in the refrigerator?
10.   Do you have any personal recommendations?
