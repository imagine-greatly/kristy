### Everything you need to add the Regenerative Organic Certified® marks to your product packaging and communications.

The Regenerative Organic Certified® Marks are the Regenerative Organic Certified mark, seals and all other trademarks, certification seals, logos, or any other proprietary designations owned by the Regenerative Organic Alliance used to indicate that a product is certified per ROA’s guidelines found in the Regenerative Organic Certified® Framework.

An organization must be licensed with the ROA to use Regenerative Organic Certified® Marks and any use must must comply with the license Agreement and the Labeling Guidelines & Terms of Use.

Please submit any use of the Regenerative Organic Certified® marks to label@regenorganic.org (label@label@regenorganic.org) for review and approval prior to printing or digital release.

Use the “®“ logos for sale of Regenerative Organic Certified products in:

- - United States (US)
- United Kingdom (UK)
- European Union (EU)
- Japan

- REGENERATIVE ORGANIC CERTIFIED® LOGOS – ENGLISH
- REGENERATIVE ORGANIC CERTIFIED® LOGOS – FRENCH/ENGLISH

For sale of products in all other countries, use the “™“ logos:

- REGENERATIVE ORGANIC CERTIFIED™ LOGOS – ENGLISH
- REGENERATIVE ORGANIC CERTIFIED™ LOGOS – FRENCH/ENGLISH
