Why It Matters

The case for grass-fed

Science-based information, not marketing. EatWild has been the trusted source for grass-fed research since 2001.

Your Health

Higher omega-3s, CLA, and antioxidants compared to grain-finished beef — real nutritional differences, backed by research.

🐄

Animal Welfare

Pasture-raised animals live as nature intended — space to roam, natural diets, and lower stress throughout their lives.

🌱

The Environment

Well-managed grazing improves soil fertility, sequesters carbon, and helps retain water and topsoil.

🚜

Farmers

Pasture-based farming supports small family farms and creates a direct relationship between land stewards and consumers.
