 Best Practices for Buying Meat Directly From Farmers

Best Practices for Buying Meat Directly From Farmers

 —

 Written by

 Bryan Hartman

 | April 15, 2020

 (Updated: Aug. 29, 2024, 11:28 a.m.)

With the public push to buy foods directly from agriculture producers and the increasing demand for local meat, farmers are stepping up to the plate to provide food for your family.  Our Agriculture & Natural Resources agent, Bryan Hartman, worked with North Carolina A&T University to create a Best Practices for Buying Meat Directly from Farmers fact sheet to help the public better understand purchasing meat products directly from a farm.
(Download the printable PDF)

Cooperative Extension at N.C. A&T State University is providing resources to promote the health and well-being of all North Carolinians. Extension is continuing to share research-based information to help people make decisions that improve their communities and their lives. More information.

For more information, contact Bryan Hartman, Agriculture and Natural Resources agent at bkhartman@ncat.edu, or Janine Woods, Ph.D., Agribusiness and Marketing Specialist, at sjparker1@ncat.edu.
