7 CFR § 205.240 - Pasture practice standard.
============================================

* [CFR](#tab_default_1)

[prev](/cfr/text/7/205.239 "Mammalian and non-avian livestock living conditions.") | [next](/cfr/text/7/205.241 "Avian living conditions.")

§ 205.240 Pasture practice standard.

The [producer](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=16e9cb7d4b810e33316a92d176695fc5&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) of an [organic](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=1292bea3254acefbcf11229e3cc1f175&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) [livestock](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=3d175a9ecb4500571fc89602b7044257&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) operation must, for all ruminant livestock on the operation, demonstrate through auditable [records](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=376ba30f313445fd782c1de3f38ce81c&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) in the [organic system plan](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=3dbdb9626bc2143abe95d66fd623f9d2&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240), a functioning management plan for [pasture](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=a6a7d01b52398ff15a5bd876376ac6ec&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240).

(a) Pasture must be managed as a [crop](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=0c1e711950695de71dab3ab708e93d2f&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) in full compliance with [§§ 205.202](/cfr/text/7/205.202), 205.203(d) and (e), 205.204, and 205.206(b) through (f). Land used for the production of annual crops for ruminant [grazing](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8eec791c76883b30acc979388f4158e8&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) must be managed in full compliance with [§§ 205.202](/cfr/text/7/205.202) through 205.206. Irrigation shall be used, as needed, to promote pasture growth when the operation has irrigation available for use on pasture.

(b) Producers must provide pasture in compliance with [§ 205.239(a)(2)](/cfr/text/7/205.239#a_2) and manage pasture to comply with the requirements of: [§ 205.237(c)(2)](/cfr/text/7/205.237#c_2), to annually provide a minimum of 30 percent of a ruminant's [dry matter intake](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=251640d6123eae50a5ef4047e4d1c6c5&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) (DMI), on average, over the course of the [grazing season](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=42f529f3bfa87d72d29f775f29c1f501&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240)(s); [§ 205.238(a)(3)](/cfr/text/7/205.238#a_3), to minimize the occurrence and spread of diseases and parasites; and [§ 205.239(e)](/cfr/text/7/205.239#e) to refrain from putting soil or water quality at risk.

(c) A pasture plan must be included in the producer's organic system plan, and be updated annually in accordance with [§ 205.406(a)](/cfr/text/7/205.406#a). The producer may resubmit the previous year's pasture plan when no change has occurred in the plan. The pasture plan may consist of a pasture/rangeland plan developed in cooperation with a Federal, [State](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=d4cb94e00feeeac2783b5194bffe4000&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240), or local conservation office: Provided, that, the submitted plan addresses all of the requirements of [§ 205.240(c)(1)](/cfr/text/7/205.240#c_1) through (8). When a change to an approved pasture plan is contemplated, which may affect the operation's compliance with the Act or the regulations in this part, the producer shall seek the [certifying agent](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=1edce589fcee127fb43c621be4e1776b&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240)'s agreement on the change prior to implementation. The pasture plan shall include a description of the:

(1) Types of pasture provided to ensure that the [feed](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=ba9e75a5d677bcc19866c084a8e0dd51&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) requirements of [§ 205.237](/cfr/text/7/205.237) are being met.

(2) Cultural and management practices to be used to ensure pasture of a sufficient quality and quantity is available to [graze](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=8ac524ad7dddf3d2d06414b0ccea60f3&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) throughout the grazing season and to provide all ruminants under the organic system plan, except exempted classes identified in [§ 205.239(c)(1)](/cfr/text/7/205.239#c_1) through (3), with an average of not less than 30 percent of their dry matter intake from grazing throughout the grazing season.

(3) Grazing season for the livestock operation's regional location.

(4) Location and size of pastures, including maps giving each pasture its own identification.

(5) The types of grazing methods to be used in the pasture system.

(6) Location and types of fences, except for temporary fences, and the location and source of shade and the location and source of water.

(7) Soil fertility and seeding systems.

(8) Erosion [control](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=b95f4eb79ebe988a3741cc0a8e6b1826&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:C:205.240) and protection of natural wetlands and riparian areas practices.

[[75 FR 7194](/rio/citation/75_FR_7194), Feb. 17, 2010]
