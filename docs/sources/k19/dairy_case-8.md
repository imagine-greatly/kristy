  1. [fairlife® ultra-filtered milk](#cat-fairlife)

     1. [How is fairlife ultra-filtered milk different than regular milk?](#faq-how-is-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-different-than-regular-milk)

        Our [ultra-filtration process](https://fairlife.com/how-we-do-it/) concentrates what makes milk great, resulting in 50% more protein and 50% less sugar compared to regular milk. This leads to a creamier, delicious taste in all our products.
     2. [Does fairlife ultra-filtered milk include added protein?](#faq-does-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-include-added-protein)

        No, we never add protein powders or concentrates. All the protein in our fairlife ultra-filtered milk comes directly from the milk. Our [ultra-filtration process](https://fairlife.com/how-we-do-it/) concentrates the natural milk proteins (80% casein and 20% whey) while removing half of the sugars compared to regular milk.
     3. [Where can I buy fairlife ultra-filtered milk?](#faq-where-can-i-buy-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk)

        Our product offerings vary by retailer. To find specific fairlife products near you, please use our [product locator](https://fairlife.com/where-to-buy/).
     4. [What flavors of fairlife ultra-filtered milk are available?](#faq-what-flavors-of-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-are-available)

        We offer fairlife ultra-filtered milk in various flavors:

        + [52oz bottles](https://fairlife.com/ultra-filtered-milk/): whole, reduced fat 2%, fat-free skim, and chocolate
        + [14oz bottles](https://fairlife.com/ultra-filtered-milk-14oz/): reduced fat 2%, chocolate, and strawberry
     5. [Is fairlife ultra-filtered milk lactose free?](#faq-is-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-lactose-free)

        Yes, thanks to our [ultra-filtration process](https://fairlife.com/how-we-do-it/) and the use of a lactase enzyme, we remove the lactose while maintaining the classic, creamy milk taste.
     6. [Is fairlife® ultra-filtered milk kosher?](#faq-is-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-kosher)

        Yes, all fairlife products are kosher.
     7. [Why does fairlife ultra-filtered milk sometimes have a smell when I open it?](#faq-why-does-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-sometimes-have-a-smell-when-i-open-it)

        A slight odor upon opening is normal due to our higher temperature pasteurization and higher protein content. This smell typically dissipates after the bottle has been open for a few minutes.
     8. [Is fairlife ultra-filtered milk organic?](#faq-is-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-organic)

        Our ultra-filtered milk is not organic, but we maintain high standards through close partnerships with our dairy suppliers, focusing on superior milk [production](https://fairlife.com/news/high-quality-standards/), sustainable farming practices, and excellent animal care.
     9. [Can I freeze fairlife ultra-filtered milk?](#faq-can-i-freeze-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-2)

        We don’t recommend freezing fairlife ultra-filtered milk, as it can alter the product’s texture and taste.
     10. [Why is fairlife ultra-filtered milk so expensive?](#faq-why-is-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-so-expensive)

         Our ultra-filtered milk offers exceptional nutritional benefits, including 50% more protein and 50% less sugar than regular milk. While prices may vary by store, fairlife is typically comparable with other premium options like organic and lactose-free milks. Keep an eye out for promotions at your favorite stores!
     11. [Why is the shelf life of ultra filtered milk longer compared to regular milk?](#faq-why-is-the-shelf-life-of-ultra-filtered-milk-longer-compared-to-regular-milk)

         We use a pasteurization process, heating our milk at a higher temperature for just a few seconds. This, combined with our carefully designed packaging, preserves our milk’s shelf life and taste much longer than conventional milk when unopened.
     12. [How long is the shelf life of fairlife ultra filtered milk?](#faq-how-long-is-the-shelf-life-of-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk)

         Thanks to our pasteurization process, fairlife ultra-filtered milk products in all sizes have a longer shelf life than conventional milk. Once opened, it should remain refrigerated and be consumed within 14 days. Always check the product packaging for up-to-date storage instructions.
     13. [Is fairlife ultra-filtered milk gluten free?](#faq-is-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-gluten-free)

         Yes, all our fairlife ultra-filtered milk products are gluten-free.
     14. [What sizes of fairlife ultra-filtered milk are available?](#faq-what-sizes-of-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-are-available)

         We offer fairlife ultra-filtered milk in [52oz bottles](https://fairlife.com/ultra-filtered-milk/) and convenient [14oz bottles](https://fairlife.com/ultra-filtered-milk-14oz/) for on-the-go consumption.
     15. [Does fairlife ultra-filtered milk come from cows treated with rBST?](#faq-does-fairlife-ultra-filtered-milk-come-from-cows-treated-with-rbst)

         No, our milk comes from cows that are not treated with rBST, also known as artificial growth hormones. While we choose not to use milk from cows treated with rBST, the FDA has stated that no significant difference has been shown between milk derived from rBST-treated and non-rBST-treated cows.
     16. [Is fairlife milk pasteurized?](#faq-is-fairlife-milk-pasteurized-2)

         Yes, all fairlife ultra-filtered milk is pasteurized for safety and quality. We use higher temperature pasteurization for a shorter time than conventional milk, which extends shelf life while maintaining our fresh, creamy taste. Learn more about how fairlife [ultra-filtered milk](https://fairlife.com/how-we-do-it/) is made.
     17. [How long does fairlife milk last after opening it?](#faq-how-long-does-fairlife-milk-last-after-opening-it)

         fairlife ultra-filtered milk lasts up to 14 days after opening when stored in the refrigerator. Before opening, our unique filtration and heating processes extend shelf life, but storage requirements vary by bottle size.

         + [**52oz bottles**](https://fairlife.com/ultra-filtered-milk/): Always keep refrigerated, even before opening.
         + [**14oz bottles**](https://fairlife.com/ultra-filtered-milk-14oz/): Can be stored at room temperature before opening but must be refrigerated after opening.
         + For both sizes, consume within 14 days of opening for best taste and quality.
         + Always check the package date for the most accurate guidance.
     18. [Can you cook or bake with fairlife milk?](#faq-can-you-cook-or-bake-with-fairlife-milk)

         Yes, use fairlife ultra-filtered milk in cooking and baking just like regular milk. Its creamy texture and rich taste enhance recipes from smoothies to baked goods. fairlife works as a one-to-one substitute in most applications. Explore new [recipes](https://fairlife.com/recipes/) you can try using fairlife.
