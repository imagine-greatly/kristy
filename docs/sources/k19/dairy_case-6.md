Pasteurized Milk Ordinance Centennial
=====================================

* [Milk Guidance Documents & Regulatory Information](/food/guidance-documents-regulatory-information-topic-food-and-dietary-supplements/milk-guidance-documents-regulatory-information)

  + [National Conference on Interstate Milk Shipments (NCIMS) Model Documents](/food/milk-guidance-documents-regulatory-information/national-conference-interstate-milk-shipments-ncims-model-documents)
  + [Pasteurized Milk Ordinance Centennial](/food/milk-guidance-documents-regulatory-information/pasteurized-milk-ordinance-centennial)

![Pasteurized Milk Ordinance Centennial](/files/styles/landscape_16_9_800_x_450/public/100MilkAnniversary_wborder_0.png?itok=_NSG-9JG "Pasteurized Milk Ordinance Centennial")

The [Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO)](/food/milk-guidance-documents-regulatory-information/national-conference-interstate-milk-shipments-ncims-model-documents "National Conference on Interstate Milk Shipments (NCIMS) Model Documents") helps to ensure that Grade “A" milk and dairy products produced in the United States are among the safest in our nation, and the world.

Originating with the Standard Milk Ordinance in 1924, it significantly reduced the risk for foodborne illness in dairy products, thus strengthening consumer confidence in the safety of the milk we drink.

Learn more about [FDA's Grade "A" Milk Safety Program](/food/federal-state-local-tribal-and-territorial-cooperative-human-food-programs/milk-safety-program-shippers-list "Milk Safety Program & Shippers List"). Check back throughout the year for additional information on this historic 100-year anniversary.
