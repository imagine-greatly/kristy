A STORY UNLIKE ANY OTHER. AN INIMITABLE MASTERPIECE.  
  
   
Like all works of art, Aceto Balsamico Tradizionale di Modena D.O.P. (Traditional Balsamic Vinegar of Modena P.D.O.) is a combination of ingenuity, knowledge and heart. It is no coincidence that it is made with the same prowess that has made Italian and Modenese art the envy of the rest of the world. The product of centuries-old traditions, the process used to make it has become an artform.

### A Modena family legacy.

As is tradition, every family ages their balsamic vinegar in batteries of wooden barrels, subjecting it to incredibly meticulous procedures and strict controls over the years. Only in this way is Traditional Balsamic Vinegar of Modena P.D.O. able to reach such a level of excellence that it elevates pleasure into an artform.

![aceto balsamico tradizionale di modena - nonno e nipote](https://www.balsamicotradizionale.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/aceto-balsamico-tradizionale-nonno-versa-a-nipote.webp)

### By true connoisseurs for true connoisseurs.

Traditional Balsamic Vinegar of Modena P.D.O. is an exclusive and inimitable product with a characteristic flavour and fragrance it is important to know how to recognise. Constantly monitored and stored by its producers, it is protected by the Consorzio (Consortium) which certifies it and promotes its qualities and use. Traditional Balsamic Vinegar of Modena P.D.O. is a product for real connoisseurs, perfect for those who love to express themselves and their lifestyles through objects, experiences and emotions, also at the dining table.

![immagine illustrativa - Aceto Balsamico Tradizionale di Modena - ABTM](https://www.balsamicotradizionale.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/AND05300-7-1.webp)

### The guarantee is in the name.

Balsamic vinegar can only call itself “Traditional Balsamic Vinegar of Modena P.D.O.” if it passes an organoleptic exam performed by a panel of expert tasters. This final step certifies its organoleptic characteristics after the long ageing period required by the Product Specifications (at least 12 years for Affinato and at least 25 years for Extravecchio) and approves its bottling and designation.

![immagine illustrativa - Aceto Balsamico Tradizionale di Modena - ABTM](https://www.balsamicotradizionale.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/AND05300-8.webp)

### Certified through to your table.

Traditional Balsamic Vinegar of Modena P.D.O. arrives on your tables only in the original bottle created by designer Giorgetto Giugiaro. The one with numbered seal and certified hologram guaranteed and controlled by the Consorzio di Tutela (Protection Consortium).
