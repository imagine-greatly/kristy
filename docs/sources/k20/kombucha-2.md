General TTB Requirements
========================

The following general requirements apply to any kombucha that contains 0.5 percent or more alcohol by volume:

* It must be produced on a premises qualified by TTB under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (IRC) (see [K16](/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/kombucha/kombucha-beer));
* It must comply with the applicable labeling, formula, and tax requirements of the IRC (see [K12](/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/kombucha/kombucha-beer), [K14](/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/kombucha/kombucha-beer), and [K15](/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/kombucha/kombucha-beer)); and
* The container must bear the health warning statement required by the Alcoholic Beverage Labeling Act of 1988 (ABLA) (see [K11](/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/kombucha/labeling)).

If a kombucha is classified as a malt beverage (as defined by the Federal Alcohol Administration Act (FAA Act)), it is also subject to the FAA Act permit, labeling, and advertising requirements (see [K13](/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/kombucha/labeling) for more information).  For information about testing methods to measure the alcohol content of kombucha, please see [K17-20](/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/kombucha/testing-methods) in the below Kombucha Information and Resources' Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs).

View the [Kombucha Information and Resources](/node/855) section to learn more.

#### IMPORTANT

TTB regulations on alcohol beverages **DO APPLY** to any kombucha that has less than 0.5 percent alcohol by volume when bottled ***BUT the alcohol content increases to 0.5 percent or more alcohol by volume at any point afterwards***as a result of continued fermentation in the bottle.

Failure to comply with federal laws and regulations governing the production, bottling, labeling, and distribution of alcohol beverages, including kombucha that contains 0.5 percent or more alcohol by volume, may lead to enforcement actions, such as the assessment of tax, penalties, and interest, in addition to potential civil and criminal penalties.
