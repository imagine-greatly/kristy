7 CFR § 205.105 - Allowed and prohibited substances, methods, and ingredients in organic production and handling.
=================================================================================================================

* [CFR](#tab_default_1)

§ 205.105 Allowed and prohibited substances, methods, and ingredients in organic production and handling.

To be sold or labeled as “100 percent [organic](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=1292bea3254acefbcf11229e3cc1f175&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:B:205.105),” “organic,” or “made with organic (specified [ingredients](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=85b11412b1e7fcf6e117efb068e6c8bd&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:B:205.105) or food group(s)),” the product must be produced and handled without the use of:

(a) [Synthetic](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=f1d94c434ae01396b0883c351df27275&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:B:205.105) substances and ingredients, except as provided in [§ 205.601](/cfr/text/7/205.601) or [§ 205.603](/cfr/text/7/205.603);

(b) Nonsynthetic substances prohibited in [§ 205.602](/cfr/text/7/205.602) or [§ 205.604](/cfr/text/7/205.604);

(c) [Nonagricultural substances](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=54bfd8d87610e38ffe7f44d31b93405a&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:B:205.105) used in or on processed products, except as otherwise provided in [§ 205.605](/cfr/text/7/205.605);

(d) Nonorganic agricultural substances used in or on processed products, except as otherwise provided in [§ 205.606](/cfr/text/7/205.606);

(e) [Excluded methods](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=28c50faa1d306000d053d30672b5838e&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:B:205.105), except for vaccines: Provided, That, the vaccines are approved in accordance with [§ 205.600(a)](/cfr/text/7/205.600#a);

(f) Ionizing radiation, as described in Food and Drug Administration regulation, [21 CFR 179.26](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/179.26); and

(g) [Sewage sludge](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=c2fa93f92b99be243598d2763f9a5046&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:7:Subtitle:B:Chapter:I:Subchapter:M:Part:205:Subpart:B:205.105).
