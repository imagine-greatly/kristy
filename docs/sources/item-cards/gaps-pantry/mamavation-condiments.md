June 9, 2016//  by [Leah Segedie](https://mamavation.com/author/mmv-admin-leah)

Let’s face it, condiments can be as important as the meal they’re on. Don’t believe me? Have you ever told a toddler you’re out of ketchup? But which condiments are best for your family? Not all ingredients are created equal. You’ve trusted Mamavation to bring you [best & worst salad dressings](https://mamavation.com/food/salad-dressing-marinade.html), [best & worst hummus](https://mamavation.com/food/hummus.html), and [best and worst chips & salsa](https://mamavation.com/food/chips-salsa.html), now join us as we investigate condiments for the safest ingredients for your family.

Condiments liven up an otherwise bland meal and give you an acceptable way to play with your food. But, before you go dipping your fries into a variety of sauce options, you should probably know what’s in that sauce.

**Would you like High Fructose Corn Syrup with that?**
------------------------------------------------------

A look at the ingredients and you may be able to spot some this one right off the bat. Many ketchups and barbecue sauces contain high fructose corn syrup (HFCS). We’ve told you before [why you should avoid HFCS at all costs](https://mamavation.com/2016/05/8-reasons-to-avoid-high-fructose-corn-syrup-at-all-costs.html). It can make you fat and even trick your brain into making you eat more. Combine that with increased cancer risk, [heart disease](https://www.ucdavis.edu/news/sugary-drinks-boost-risk-factors-heart-disease-study-shows), and that it can [actually make you stupid](http://newsroom.ucla.edu/releases/this-is-your-brain-on-sugar-ucla-233992), it’s not good eats.

![Bite Me Best Foods Mayo ](https://mamavation.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/how-does-your-favorite-sauce-stack-up-condiments-from-bad-to-best-7.jpg)

**What’s Inside the Secret Sauce? Preservatives & Probable Carcinogens**
------------------------------------------------------------------------

As you scroll through the list of ingredients, there are some that may be hiding a secret, though. Natural smoke flavor, for example, may sound harmless, but [research found it to damage the stomach lining of rats and cause DNA damage.](http://www.cnn.com/2013/03/29/health/time-food-flavorings/) The preservative calcium disodium EDTA is linked to allergic reactions, skin rashes, and possibly even kidney damage.

Spice, natural flavoring, maltodextrin, modified food starch, and enzyme-modified egg yolk can all be [hidden msg ingredients](https://mamavation.com/2015/04/90-hidden-msg-ingredients.html). MSG is an excitotoxin that tricks the brain into thinking food tastes better. High levels of MSG have been linked to numerous diseases, learning disorders, and even brain damage.

![Ketchup and mustard squiggles on the table](https://mamavation.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/how-does-your-favorite-sauce-stack-up-condiments-from-bad-to-best-3.jpg)

I also spotted genetically modified ingredients in many condiments. Currently, there is no mandatory labeling of GMOs required in the US, so until more brands decide to label them, it’s tough to know for sure. Glyphosate, the most common pesticide sprayed on GMO crops, [is a probable carcinogen](http://www.iarc.fr/en/media-centre/iarcnews/pdf/MonographVolume112.pdf) according to the World Health Organization. With [over 90% of corn and soybean crops](http://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/adoption-of-genetically-engineered-crops-in-the-us/recent-trends-in-ge-adoption.aspx) grown in the US being genetically modified, it’s safe to assume ingredients derived from these crops are GMO.

Therefore, condiments containing canola oil, soybean oil, corn syrup, and HFCS are all likely to contain GMOs. Even “sugar” indicated sugar from likely GM sugar beets, not cane sugar.

![mustard with mustard seeds on a green table cloth](https://mamavation.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/how-does-your-favorite-sauce-stack-up-condiments-from-bad-to-best-4.jpg)
