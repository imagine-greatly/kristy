§ 184.1854 Sucrose.

(a) Sucrose (C12H22O11, [CAS Reg. No. 57-50-11](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/search/#query=57-50-11)-1) sugar, cane sugar, or beet sugar is the chemical β-D-fructofuranosyl-α-D-glucopyranoside. Sucrose is obtained by crystallization from sugar cane or sugar beet juice that has been extracted by pressing or diffusion, then clarified and evaporated.

(b) The ingredient must be of a purity suitable for its intended use.

(c) In accordance with [§ 184.1(b)(1)](/cfr/text/21/184.1#b_1), the ingredient is used in [food](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=5f3b7ccf4b3f4f15bfde1fba6edad879&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:21:Chapter:I:Subchapter:B:Part:184:Subpart:B:184.1854) with no limitation other than current good manufacturing practice.

(d) [Prior sanctions](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=81b4e43f32e8aad93e7cf4258ef0a338&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:21:Chapter:I:Subchapter:B:Part:184:Subpart:B:184.1854) for this ingredient different from the uses established in this section do not exist or have been waived.
