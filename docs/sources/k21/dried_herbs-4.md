§ 182.10 Spices and other natural seasonings and flavorings.

Spices and other natural seasonings and flavorings that are generally recognized as [safe](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=5bd83dddb4d1664267b046a540c5e455&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:21:Chapter:I:Subchapter:B:Part:182:Subpart:A:182.10) for their intended use, within the meaning of section 409 of the Act, are as follows:
