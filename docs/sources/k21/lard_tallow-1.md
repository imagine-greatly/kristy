§ 319.702 Lard, leaf lard.

(a) Lard is the fat rendered from clean and sound [edible](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=35a88203112de0b8bb42c5858c0c8345&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:9:Chapter:III:Subchapter:A:Part:319:Subpart:P:319.702) tissues from swine. The tissues may be fresh, frozen, cooked, or [prepared](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=e8c343c40e2198b8e0b15b8cb9b8465e&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:9:Chapter:III:Subchapter:A:Part:319:Subpart:P:319.702) by other processes approved by the Administrator in specific cases, upon his determination that the use of such processes will not result in the adulteration or misbranding of the lard. The tissues shall be reasonably free from blood, and shall not include stomachs, livers, spleens, kidneys, and brains, or settlings and skimmings. “Leaf Lard” is lard prepared from fresh leaf (abdominal) fat.

(b) Lard (when properly labeled) may be hardened by the use of lard stearin or hydrogenated lard or both and may contain refined lard and deodorized lard, but the [labels](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=b340477127061797fa91f28e701612e2&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:9:Chapter:III:Subchapter:A:Part:319:Subpart:P:319.702) of such lard shall [state](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=0f4f8b11b2110e6e7ad228d3a3317ca4&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:9:Chapter:III:Subchapter:A:Part:319:Subpart:P:319.702) such facts, as applicable.

(c) [Products](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=3ea373d917d7b33d7c877302099e293b&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:9:Chapter:III:Subchapter:A:Part:319:Subpart:P:319.702) labeled “Lard” or “Leaf Lard” must have the following identity and quality characteristics to insure good color, odor, and taste of finished product:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| (1) Color | White when solid, Maximum 3.0 red units in a 5 1/4 inch cell on the Lovibond scale. |
| (2) Odor and taste | Characteristic and free from foreign odors and flavors. |
| (3) Free fatty acid | Maximum 0.5 percent (as oleic) or 1.0 acid value, as milligrams KOH per gram of sample. |
| (4) Peroxide value | Maximum 5.0 (as milliequivalents of peroxide per kilogram fat). |
| (5) Moisture and volatile matter | Maximum 0.2 percent. |
| (6) Insoluble impurities | By appearance of liquid, fat or maximum 0.05 percent. |

(d) Product found upon inspection not to have the characteristics specified in [paragraph (c)](#c) of this section but found to be otherwise sound and in compliance with [paragraph (a)](#a) of this section may be further processed for the purpose of achieving such characteristics.
