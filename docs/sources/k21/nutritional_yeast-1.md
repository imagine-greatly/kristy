§ 172.896 Dried yeasts.

Dried yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae and Saccharomyces fragilis) and dried torula yeast (Candida utilis) may be safely used in [food](/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=5f3b7ccf4b3f4f15bfde1fba6edad879&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:21:Chapter:I:Subchapter:B:Part:172:Subpart:I:172.896) provided the total folic acid content of the yeast does not exceed 0.04 milligram per gram of yeast (approximately 0.008 milligram of pteroyglutamic acid per gram of yeast).
