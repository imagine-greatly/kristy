# EWG’s Dirty Dozen™ Guide to Food Chemicals: The top 12 to avoid

Our food should be nourishing and safe to eat.

But more than 10,000 chemicals, some of which are potentially toxic, are allowed in cereal, snacks, meat and many other types of food sold in the U.S.

[**Almost 99 percent**](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2022/04/ewg-analysis-almost-all-new-food-chemicals-greenlighted-industry-not-fda) of food chemicals introduced since 2000 were greenlighted for use by food and chemical companies rather than properly reviewed by the Food and Drug Administration. Many of these widely used chemicals are associated with major health harms, including increased risk of cancer, developmental harm and hormone disruption.

These substances end up in what we eat, thanks to a legal loophole that allows food ingredients to be classified as “ [**generally recognized as safe**](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-ingredients-packaging/generally-recognized-safe-gras#:~:text=%22GRAS%22%20is%20an%20acronym%20for,phrase%20Generally%20Recognized%20As%20Safe. "(opens in a new window)")(link is external).” The loophole lets manufacturers – instead of the FDA – determine which food chemicals are safe to consume. Chemical and food companies have exploited this loophole for decades.

That can leave consumers struggling to know what’s safe for their families.

By highlighting some of the worst offenders on the market, EWG’s Dirty Dozen™ Guide to Food Chemicals shows you which chemicals in food to avoid:

![Baked goods](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2022-07/EWG_Card_DD-FoodChem_02_C01.jpg?h=52d3fcb6&itok=O3XUcS0L)

Potassium bromate

[Potassium bromate](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2022/05/cancer-causing-chemical-may-be-lurking-your-bread) is a possible human carcinogen added to flour used in packaged baked goods.

![Tortillas](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2022-07/EWG_Card_DD-FoodChem_03_C01.jpg?h=52d3fcb6&itok=YZGK_AGh)

Propyl paraben

[Propyl paraben](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2025/04/what-propyl-paraben) is a preservative used in pastries and some tortillas. It causes developmental and reproductive harm.

![Potato chips](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2022-07/EWG_Card_DD-FoodChem_04_C01.jpg?h=52d3fcb6&itok=nzYzZb55)

BHA

[Butylated hydroxyanisole](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2026/06/dirty-dozentm-food-chemicals-bha), better known as BHA, is a preservative used in cured meats and other foods. Multiple sources have identified it as a possible human carcinogen.

![Fruit loops](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2022-07/EWG_Card_DD-FoodChem_05_C01.jpg?h=52d3fcb6&itok=3QzY2k4O)

BHT

[Butylated hydroxytoluene](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2026/06/dirty-dozen-food-chemicals-bht), also called BHT, is a preservative found in cereals and other foods. It’s a chemical cousin of BHA and is a possible human carcinogen.

![Candy](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2022-07/EWG_Card_DD-FoodChem_07_C01.jpg?h=52d3fcb6&itok=Ib3knm1l)

Titanium dioxide

[Titanium dioxide](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2021/08/thousands-childrens-sweets-contain-additive-unsafe-human-consumption) is a color additive used mostly in candy. It may damage DNA.

![Pop tarts](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2022-07/EWG_Card_DD-FoodChem_06_C01.jpg?h=52d3fcb6&itok=JKjaxoBC)

Seven artificial dyes

[Synthetic food dye](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2021/04/california-agency-acknowledges-synthetic-food-dyes-link-hyperactivity) can be found in many types of food and beverages. It can affect development and cause behavioral difficulties in children. There are seven dyes that are especially concerning: Red 3, Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6, Blue 1, Blue 2 and Green 3.

![Soda](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2024-09/ArtificialSweetnerBlog.jpg?h=c673cd1c&itok=aKRkcSmX)

Aspartame

[Aspartame](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2026/08/dirty-dozentm-food-chemicals-aspartame) is an artificial sweetener that can affect the hormones that regulate metabolism and have a negative effect on weight control. It has also been linked to heart disease and increased risk of cancer.

![Baked goods](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2022-07/EWG_Card_DD-FoodChem_02_C01.jpg?h=52d3fcb6&itok=O3XUcS0L)

ADA

[Azodicarbonamide](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2026/08/dirty-dozentm-food-chemicals-ada), or ADA, is used as a dough conditioner and strengthener, as well as a bleaching agent in breads and pastries. ADA has been linked to an increased risk of cancer. It may also cause liver and kidney injury and affect the blood and the nervous and respiratory systems.

![Child chewing gum](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2023-07/MintGumBlog.jpg?h=2e181f1f&itok=3LD12xp1)

Propyl gallate

[Propyl gallate](https://www.ewg.org/research/ewg-evaluation-food-chemicals-propyl-gallate) is a preservative used in vegetable oil, meat products and chewing gum, among other products. It may cause hormone disruption and has been linked to an increased risk of asthma, hyperactivity and even cancer.

![Close-up photograph of golden, salted potato chips](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2026-04/tbhqblog1.jpg?h=2e181f1f&itok=PgQQ1Uzu)

TBHQ

[TBHQ](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2026/04/dirty-dozen-food-chemicals-tbhq) is a preservative that prevents oxidation. Also known as tertiary butylhydroquinone, it is typically added to packaged foods like crackers, potato chips and baked goods containing oil. Many animal studies have associated TBHQ with immune dysfunction, including potential impairment of the body’s ability to regulate inflammation and fight infections and cancer.

![Macro photograph of amber liquid with suspended bubbles and textured surface](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2026-04/sucraloseblog2.jpg?h=2e181f1f&itok=521aVH-b)

Sucralose

[Sucralose](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2026/04/dirty-dozen-food-chemicals-sucralose) is an artificial sweetener that is about 600 times sweeter than sugar. Non-sugar sweeteners like sucralose may increase risk of Type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease and mortality in adults, as well as preterm birth.

![Charcuterie board](https://www.ewg.org/sites/default/files/styles/standard_sm/public/2021-11/CharcuterieBlog.jpg?h=2e181f1f&itok=9E6LjypL)

Sodium nitrite

[Sodium nitrite](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news/2024/09/what-are-sodium-nitrite-and-sodium-nitrate) is an additive used to preserve meat. When added to processed foods, nitrites are associated with an increased risk of cancer.
