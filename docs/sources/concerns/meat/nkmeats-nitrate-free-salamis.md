# Celebrate Tradition with Na\*Kyrsie's Nitrate Free Salami

## A Taste of Salami's Rich History

At Na\*Kyrsie, we honor the timeless tradition of salami-making with our commitment to quality and taste. Like our famous nitrate-free pepperoni, our nitrate-free salamis are crafted using simple, pure ingredients and dried and aged with techniques that enhance the meat’s natural flavors.

## Our Nitrate Free Salami Varieties

### Nitrate Free Toscano Salami

A lean artisan pork salami spiced with red wine and fennel seeds, Toscano salami offers a rich texture and robust flavor. Its peppery, garlicky notes make it ideal for antipasti, salads, or sandwiches, and it pairs beautifully with wine and cheese.

[SHOP](https://www.nkmeats.com/product-page/nitrate-free-toscano-salami)

### Nitrate Free Cacciatore Salami

A blend of pork and beef seasoned with coriander and cayenne, Cacciatore salami delivers a savory, spicy flavor perfect for hearty snacks and sandwiches.

[SHOP](https://www.nkmeats.com/product-page/nitrate-free-cacciatore-salami)

### Nitrate Free Soppressata

This traditional Italian-style pork salami is seasoned with garlic, white wine, and red pepper flakes. Its vibrant red color, marbled texture, and balanced heat make it a standout on any charcuterie board.

[SHOP](https://www.nkmeats.com/product-page/nitrate-free-soppresate)

### Nitrate Free Manzo Salami

Crafted from beef and subtly spiced, Manzo salami offers a robust, savory flavor and a pork-free alternative that’s perfect for sandwiches or charcuterie boards.

[SHOP](https://www.nkmeats.com/product-page/nitrate-free-manzo-salami)

## Surprising Ways to Enjoy Nitrate Free Salami

While nitrate-free salami is a classic sandwich and charcuterie board staple, its bold flavor makes it perfect for a variety of dishes. Crisp up uncured salami in a pan and sprinkle it over salads, pasta, or roasted vegetables for a savory crunch. You can also mix diced salami into omelets, pair it with honey and figs for a sweet-and-salty contrast, or use any variety on a homemade pizza. However you use it, Na\*Kyrsie’s nitrate-free salamis bring deep, artisanal flavor to every bite.
