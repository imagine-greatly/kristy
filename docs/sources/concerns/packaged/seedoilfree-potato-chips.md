# Do Potato Chips Contain Seed Oils?

Published September 26, 2025

![Do Potato Chips Contain Seed Oils?](https://images.seedoilfreecertified.com/news/news_84.webp)

Everyday Foods

Most potato chips are fried in seed oils such as sunflower, canola, soybean, or corn oil. Here is what to know when shopping for chips.

## Quick Answer

Most potato chips contain seed oils because they are fried during manufacturing. Common oils include sunflower oil, canola oil, soybean oil, corn oil, and blended vegetable oils.

The type of oil used varies by brand, cooking method, and product positioning.

## Why Seed Oils Are Commonly Used in Chips

Manufacturers rely on seed oils because they are inexpensive, scalable, and capable of handling high frying temperatures. Industrial chip production requires oils that can consistently produce crisp texture and long shelf life.

Even kettle-cooked or “natural” potato chips frequently contain refined seed oils.

## Common Oils Found in Potato Chips

### Most Common

- Sunflower oil
- Canola oil
- Soybean oil
- Corn oil

### Marketing Terms

- Vegetable oil
- [High oleic oils](https://www.seedoilfreecertified.com/seed-oils-guide/ingredient-deep-dives/what-is-high-oleic-sunflower-oil)
- Kettle cooked
- Avocado oil chips

Some products use blended oils, making ingredient labels especially important to review.

## How To Read Potato Chip Labels

Consumers should look carefully for soybean oil, sunflower oil, safflower oil, canola oil, corn oil, or generic vegetable oil terminology.

Some specialty chip brands now prominently advertise avocado oil or olive oil alternatives.

## Better Alternatives

Consumers looking to reduce seed oil intake may prefer chips cooked in avocado oil, olive oil, or coconut oil. Homemade potato chips also allow greater control over ingredients and cooking fats.

See also our guides to [foods that do not contain seed oils](https://www.seedoilfreecertified.com/seed-oils-guide/everyday-foods/foods-that-do-not-contain-seed-oils) and [reading ingredient labels](https://www.seedoilfreecertified.com/seed-oils-guide/practical-guides/how-to-identify-seed-oils-on-ingredient-labels).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do potato chips contain seed oils?

Most potato chips are fried in seed oils such as sunflower, canola, soybean, or corn oil.

### Why are seed oils used for potato chips?

Seed oils are inexpensive, scalable, and effective for commercial frying.

### Are there potato chips made without seed oils?

Some brands use avocado oil, olive oil, coconut oil, or animal fats instead.

### Why are snack foods associated with seed oils?

Snack foods heavily rely on refined oils for texture, crispness, and manufacturing efficiency.
