## Description

Our 100% Grass-fed Beef Ancestral ™ Blend is ground beef supercharged with liver and heart, delivering incredible flavor and serious nutritional benefits from grass-fed organ meats. It looks, feels, and tastes remarkably similar to regular ground beef. The difference? It’s packed with even more nutrients that support energy, muscle, focus, hormones, and mood–think B12, creatine, collagen, iron, zinc, and more. This isn’t just another protein, it’s an effortless upgrade to your health that’s quick and easy to cook. Perfect for burgers, tacos, or any meal where you would use ground beef—say hello to your new household staple.

- Each package is 16 oz
- A blend of 80% lean and 20% fat
- Ingredients: Beef, beef liver, and beef heart
- Contains 8% organ meats for added nutrition and depth of flavor.
- Regeneratively raised. 100% Grass-fed and grass-finished.
