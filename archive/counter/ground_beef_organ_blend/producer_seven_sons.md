### Product Details

Nourish your body the way our ancestors did without giving up the convenience of ground beef. Our primal ground beef makes it easy to incorporate organ meats into your family's diet without anyone noticing.

### Ancestral Nutrition With Familiar Ground Beef Flavor

We've perfected our Primal Blend: 92% ground beef, 5% beef liver, and 3% beef heart. The ratio is carefully calibrated to deliver the nutritional benefits of organ meats, while our grass-fed ground beef delivers all the flavor.

### What Is Primal Blend Ground Beef?

This primal ground beef combines grass-fed ground beef with nutrient-dense organs, following the nose-to-tail eating philosophy practiced for millennia. Each 16-oz package contains an 85/15 lean-to-fat ratio with the same cooking performance you expect from quality ground beef.

### Packed with Bioavailable Nutrients

Beef liver and heart are among the most nutrient-dense foods available. Our ground beef with liver provides exceptional vitamin A, B12, iron, folate, and copper—all in forms your body actually absorbs. The heart contributes CoQ10 and B vitamins for energy and cardiovascular health.

### From 100% Grass-Fed, Grass-Finished Cattle

Every ingredient comes from cattle raised on our regenerative pastures, grazing fresh grass their entire lives. We never use grain finishing, hormones, antibiotics, or steroids. When you're eating liver and heart, clean sourcing matters—organs filter what animals eat. For more options, explore our [grass-fed beef](https://sevensons.net/store/grass-fed-beef) selection.

### Perfect for Carnivore and Paleo Diets

Our Primal Blend Ground Beef is a staple in carnivore and paleo communities because it delivers the nutrient density these approaches prioritize. We've made this ancestral way of eating effortless; there's no need to prepare liver separately or convince your family to try something unfamiliar. If you're interested in exploring more beef organ options, browse our [grass-fed beef organs](https://sevensons.net/store/grass-fed-beef/organs).

### Primal Blend vs. Regular Ground Beef

Nutritionally, our Primal Blend delivers significantly higher vitamins and minerals thanks to the added organ meats. The taste, texture, and cooking methods are identical to regular ground beef. Both options come from our 100% grass-fed and grass-finished cattle raised on regenerative pastures. Choose our [regular ground beef](https://sevensons.net/store/product/ground-beef) for everyday meals, and reach for Primal Blend when you want to maximize nutrition—especially for active lifestyles, recovery, or growing kids.

### Use It in Any Ground Beef Recipe

Brown it for tacos, shape it into burgers, roll it into meatballs, or simmer it in chili. This primal ground beef works in any recipe that calls for ground beef. We use it interchangeably in our own kitchens because there’s no need to adjust our favorite recipes.

### How to Cook Primal Ground Beef

Cook to 160°F, just like traditional ground beef. Brown it in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into crumbles, or form patties for the grill. No special handling needed, and you can season it however you would normally enjoy ground beef.

### Storage and Preparation

Your ground beef with organ meat arrives frozen and stays fresh in your freezer for up to one year. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator and cook within three days. Stock up with our [curated bundles](https://sevensons.net/store/value-bundles) to always have clean, nutrient-dense protein on hand.

### Common Questions About our Ground Beef- Primal Blend

What is ancestral ground beef?

Ancestral ground beef is a term used for a blend of ground beef and organ meats. Our Primal Blend is 92% ground beef, 5% beef liver, and 3% beef heart from 100% grass-fed andgrass-finished cattle. It combines the convenience and familiar taste of ground beef with the superior nutrition of organ meats.



Does primal ground beef taste different from regular ground beef?

No, our Primal Blend tastes identical to regular ground beef. We've carefully calibrated the ratio, adding only 5% liver and 3% heart, so the organs don’t change the beef's flavor. In our experience serving this blend to families, no one can detect any difference in taste or texture.



What are the benefits of beef liver and heart in ground beef?

Beef liver provides bioavailable vitamin A, B12, iron, folate, and copper for energy, immunity, and vitality. Beef heart contributes CoQ10 and B vitamins for cardiovascular and metabolic health. Together, these organs deliver nutrient density you can't get from muscle meat alone.



How do you cook primal ground beef?

Cook it like traditional ground beef—no special preparation needed. Brown it in a skillet over medium-high heat or form patties for grilling. Always cook to 160°F. The blend takes seasoning beautifully and retains moisture well.



Is Primal Blend ground beef good for carnivore diet?

Yes, and we've seen it become a staple for folks eating this way. The nose-to-tail approach means getting nutrients from organs that you can't get enough of from muscle meat alone. This blend gives you those benefits without having to prepare liver separately.



Can I use Primal Blend in any ground beef recipe?

Absolutely. This blend works in any recipe that calls for ground beef: tacos, burgers, meatballs, chili, meat sauce, or casseroles. It cooks the same as conventional ground beef. We use it interchangeably in our own kitchens and never adjust cooking methods or recipes.



How should I store primal ground beef?

Your Primal Blend ground beef arrives frozen and stays fresh in your freezer for up to one year. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator and cook within three days. Treat it like any quality ground beef—no special storage or handling required.
