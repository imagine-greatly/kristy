The Hateful Eight
Cate Shanahan, MD, coined the term The Hateful Eight bad seed oils to identify the fats that are making us sick. The Hateful Eight Vegetable seed oils are: Corn, Canola, Cottonseed, Soy, Sunflower, Safflower, Rice bran, and Grapeseed.
I first identified this list of eight problem oils in my 2010 book Food Rules . But I coined the name ‘The Hateful Eight’ for a 2022 interview with Megyn Kelly, publishing the term on DrCate.com that same year and later featuring it in Dark Calories
The average American gets 80% of their fat calories from these eight seed oils, making them the single largest source of calories in most people’s diets. Yet doctors and dietitians learn very little about the health harms of these oils. In fact, dietitians learn that they are heart-healthy!
Dietitians are not taught that these eight oils contain unstable fatty acids that can break down into toxins and that nature protects them with an array of nutrients but that the harsh factory refining removes these protective factors. It also removes important nutrients including vitamins and minerals. Dietitians will downplay that because the complex chemistry of fatty acid oxidation is not part of the standard education on these fats; I wouldn’t know this had I not studied biochemistry at Cornell University.
The extraction (even organic, expeller pressed) and the refining cause many problems because they generate toxicity and remove nutrients. Those missing nutrients render fragile polyunsaturated fatty acids vulnerable to oxidation when exposed to light, when you open the bottle, when you cook with them, and when you reheat them. This problem is pushed under the rug and nobody is talking about it. If you’d like to learn more about their health effects, visit this page https://drcate.com/pufa-project/
