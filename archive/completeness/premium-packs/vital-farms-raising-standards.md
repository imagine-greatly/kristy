Raising the Standards
Building A Resilient Food System
By scaling family farms, prioritizing animal welfare, and raising the standards of what's available on grocery shelves, we're proving the food industry wrong.
One egg at a time.

How We Operate
We prioritize the long-term benefits of those impacted by the decisions we make. It's not easy, but we believe it's worth it.

Decisions for Longevity
Stakeholder Model
We make decisions that prioritize the long-term benefit to our stakeholders: our farmers, crew members, customers, vendors, investors, the communities we touch, our girls on grass, and the environment.
in Business for Good
Certified B Corporation
B Corps are for-profit companies dedicated to using their business as a force for good. Becoming a B Corp was a natural extension of the way we do business.
Balancing profit and people
Conscious Capitalism
We balance profitability with the stakeholder model focused on people, animals, and the planet. We consider everyone affected by our decisions, not just the bottom line.

Hens & Farmers
We give our girls on grass the outdoor lifestyle they deserve — meaning access to fresh air and sunshine, year-round. We work with farmers who are as passionate about animal welfare as we are, compensate them fairly, and provide ongoing, in-person support through our Farmer Support Crew.

Crew
Our crew members are a big part of what makes our mission possible. From Egg Central Station to the home offices of our remote crew, we do our best to foster an empowering work environment and provide them with opportunities to grow.

Vendors & Communities
Across Austin, Texas (our hometown), The Pasture Belt, and beyond, we strive to be a consistent and reliable partner to those we work with and the communities we operate in.

The Environment
We strive to leave the land better than we found it, and do so by implementing practices like pasture rotation on all our farms.

Consumers
By supporting our mission, you continue to show the industry that people crave food they can trust.

Shareholders
Our shareholders believe in our model, which allows us to confidently grow our business with the intent of doing good.
