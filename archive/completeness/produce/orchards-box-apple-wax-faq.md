grows where.\n\nAre your apples waxed or chemically treated?\n\nNo — our [Himachal apples](/pages/shop-fresh-apples-fruit-online) are never waxed or chemically coated.\n\nSupermarket apples are often coated in edible wax to look shiny after months in cold storage. Ours go from the orchard to your door within days, so they need no wax at all. What you see is the apple's own natural skin, sometimes with a light bloom — a sign of freshness, not a flaw.\n\nWhy don't your apples shine like supermarket apples?\n\nBecause there is no wax on them.\n\nThat mirror shine you see in supermarkets comes from a food-grade wax coating applied before apples enter cold storage. Our wax-free Himachal apples keep their natural matte skin and a faint white bloom the tree itself produces. A duller apple picked this week will always taste better than a polished one picked months ago.\n\nWhat does “no cold storage” actually mean?\n\nIt means your apples are never held in a chilled warehouse before they reach you.\n\nMost apples sold in India spend three to eight months in cold storage after harvest. We skip that step entirely — our farmers pick your fruit during the [harvest week you choose](/pages/shop-fresh-apples-fruit-online), and we dispatch within 48 hours. That is also why we can only sell in season.\n\nWhat's the difference between Kinnaur apples and Shimla apples?\n\nAltitude, mostly.\n\nKinnaur apples grow higher and colder, often above 2,000 metres, which slows ripening and concentrates sugar — giving a denser, crisper apple with deeper red colour. Shimla apples grow slightly lower and warmer, ripening a little earlier with a softer, more aromatic sweetness. We grow our [Red Delicious](/products/red-delicious) in Kinnaur, and our [Golden](/products/golden-apples), [Granny Smith](/products/granny-smith-apples) and [Red Golden](/products/red-golden-apples) apples in Shimla.\n\nWhat's the difference between Red Delicious, Golden and Royal Delicious?\n\nThey differ in colour, texture and sweetness.\n\n[Red Delicious](/products/red-delicious) is deep red, crisp and classically sweet — our Kinnaur variety. [Golden apples](/products/golden-apples) are yellow-green, softer and honey-sweet, gentle for children and elders. [Red Golden](/products/red-golden-apples) is a two-tone apple balancing the two: Golden's sweetness with a firmer bite. Royal Delicious is a closely related strain of the same Delicious family grown across Himachal; we currently sell Red Delicious rather than Royal.\n\nAre your apples organic?\n\nOur apples are not certified organic, and we would rather say so plainly.\n\nWhat we can promise is that they are wax-free, never chemically coated, and never cold-stored. Our Himachal farming families grow with minimal intervention, but organic certification is a costly, multi-year process most small hill farmers cannot afford.
