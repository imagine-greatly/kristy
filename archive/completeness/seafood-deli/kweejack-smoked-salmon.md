 Smoked salmon can refer to many different products—from farm-raised Atlantic salmon to wild Pacific salmon and from thinly sliced cold-smoked salmon to fully cooked hot-smoked portions. The species of salmon, where it comes from, how it is handled, and how it is smoked all affect the finished product.

At Kwee-Jack Fish Co., our smoked salmon begins with wild-caught Alaskan sockeye salmon. Sockeye is naturally known for its deep red color, firm texture, and distinctive salmon flavor, making it especially well suited for smoking.

We offer both Nova cold-smoked wild sockeye salmon and traditional hot-smoked wild sockeye salmon. Although both begin with wild Alaskan sockeye, the different smoking methods create two very different textures and eating experiences.

Why Wild Alaskan Sockeye Makes Exceptional Smoked Salmon

Not all smoked salmon begins with the same species of fish. Our smoked salmon is made from wild-caught Alaskan sockeye salmon, rather than farm-raised Atlantic salmon.

Sockeye is one of Alaska's best-known wild salmon species. Its naturally deep red flesh, firm texture, and pronounced salmon flavor hold up particularly well during the brining and smoking process.

That stronger natural character is one reason we prefer sockeye for our smoked salmon. The goal isn't to cover up the flavor of the fish with smoke. Instead, the brining and smoking process complements the flavor of the wild salmon itself.

Where Does Our Wild Alaskan Smoked Salmon Come From?

Kwee-Jack is a commercial fishing family, and wild Alaskan salmon is at the heart of our business. Our family has spent years commercial fishing in Alaska, giving us firsthand experience with wild salmon, seafood handling, and the importance of quality from harvest through processing.

Our smoked salmon is made from wild-caught Alaskan sockeye salmon and custom smoked for Kwee-Jack by a nationally award-winning smokehouse.

For us, the quality of smoked salmon starts before the fish ever reaches the smokehouse. The species, origin, handling, freezing, and processing of the salmon all contribute to the finished product.

Cold-Smoked vs. Hot-Smoked Wild Salmon

One of the most common points of confusion when shopping for smoked salmon is the difference between cold-smoked salmon and hot-smoked salmon.

Both involve smoke, but they are prepared differently and have noticeably different textures.

Nova Cold-Smoked Wild Alaskan Sockeye

Our Nova-style sockeye is delicately brined, skinned, and slowly cold-smoked. It is then thinly sliced, producing the silky, smooth texture many people associate with traditional Nova-style smoked salmon.

The finished salmon has a delicate texture and refined smoky flavor. It is ready to eat and works especially well with bagels, cream cheese, eggs, crackers, appetizers, salads, and brunch dishes.

Traditional Hot-Smoked Wild Alaskan Sockeye

Our traditional hot-smoked sockeye is brined, portioned, and smoked using carefully controlled heat. The term “hot-smoked” describes the smoking method—it does not mean the salmon is spicy.

Hot smoking produces a fully cooked piece of salmon with a tender, flaky texture and richer smoky character. It can be eaten directly from the package or added to salads, appetizers, eggs, pasta, dips, and other dishes.

If you're deciding which style you prefer, you can compare our Nova cold-smoked and traditional hot-smoked wild Alaskan sockeye salmon options, both available for frozen home delivery.

How Is Our Wild Alaskan Smoked Salmon Made?

Both styles begin with wild Alaskan sockeye salmon and a simple brine. The salmon is then smoked using natural wood smoke, but the smoking process differs depending on the style being produced.

Cold smoking uses a different temperature and process to create the smooth, delicate texture of Nova-style salmon. Hot smoking uses controlled heat during the smoking process, producing fully cooked salmon with a flaky texture.

Both styles are custom smoked for Kwee-Jack by a nationally award-winning smokehouse and are packaged frozen to help preserve quality until you're ready to enjoy them.

What's in Our Smoked Salmon?

We believe a good piece of smoked salmon doesn't require a complicated ingredient list.

Both our Nova cold-smoked and traditional hot-smoked wild sockeye are made with:

Wild Alaskan sockeye salmon (fish)

Organic dark brown sugar

Sea salt

Natural wood smoke

No nitrates or preservatives are added.

The brine and natural wood smoke complement the salmon rather than replacing its natural flavor.

What Does Wild Alaskan Smoked Salmon Taste Like?

Wild sockeye has a distinctive salmon flavor even before it is smoked. Smoking adds savory, salty, and smoky notes while allowing the character of the salmon to remain noticeable.

The exact eating experience depends considerably on which smoking style you choose.

Nova cold-smoked sockeye is smooth, silky, and delicate, with a subtle smoky character. Because it is thinly sliced, it works especially well when paired with other flavors and textures.

Traditional hot-smoked sockeye has a firmer, flaky texture and a more substantial bite. Its smoky flavor works well on its own or as part of a meal.

Neither style is intended to be spicy. “Hot” in hot-smoked salmon refers to the smoking process rather than the flavor.

Is Wild-Caught Smoked Salmon Different From Farm-Raised Smoked Salmon?

Yes. The underlying fish is different before the smoking process even begins.

Our smoked salmon is made from wild Alaskan sockeye salmon. Sockeye lives and feeds naturally in the wild and is a different species from the farm-raised Atlantic salmon commonly used for many smoked salmon products.

Wild sockeye is typically recognized for its deep natural color, firm flesh, and pronounced salmon flavor. Those characteristics carry through into the finished smoked salmon and are part of why we choose wild sockeye for our product.

If you'd like to learn more about wild salmon species, sourcing, sustainability, cooking, and storage, visit our Complete Wild Salmon Buyer's & Cooking Guide.

Why Kwee-Jack Chooses Wild Alaskan Sockeye for Smoked Salmon

Kwee-Jack Fish Co. started with commercial fishing, not simply selling seafood online. Our family personally harvests wild salmon in Alaska, and that experience influences how we think about every seafood product we offer.

For smoked salmon, we want the fish itself to be something worth smoking. That's why we use wild Alaskan sockeye salmon and have it custom smoked rather than treating smoking as a way to disguise an ordinary piece of fish.

We also believe customers should know what they're buying. We identify the salmon species, explain the difference between our cold-smoked and hot-smoked products, list the ingredients, and provide information about how the product is packaged and handled.
