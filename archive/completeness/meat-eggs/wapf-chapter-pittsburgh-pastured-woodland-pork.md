Blackberry Meadows Farm
Blackberry Meadows Farm is a Certified Organic fruit and vegetable CSA and market farm which also produces pastured non-GMO pork, beef, and poultry. They serve their neighbors in the Alle-Kiski Valley with fresh meat and vegetables from their barn store and serve the Pittsburgh Region with markets, Garden Shares, and volunteer food systems leadership.
They are a uniquely ‘open-door’ farm, which provides their membership community with access to both nutrient dense, fresh, organic, local produce, AND the opportunity to join them in the fields and learn how to produce your own. Their customers receive the highest quality, most nutrient dense fuel for their families.
7115 Ridge Road, Natrona Heights, PA 15065

Footprints Farms
Jeremy and Ellen Swartzfager run a sustainably-designed farm raising premium meats and produce for local restaurants and families. All of their animals are raised on pasture with plenty of sunshine and fresh grass to graze, eat, or root through each day. They use draft animal traction power, practice rotational multi-species grazing, and employ time tested crop rotations to provide the most nutritionally rich meat and veggies.
Fayette County

Green Circle Delivers

North Woods Ranch
North Woods Ranch is run by Oliver and Jodi Griswold and their family providing free-range pastured Scottish Highlands beef and Berkshire hogs on 180 acres of land in Marshall Township. The owners say, “Our operations are deeply rooted to our personal commitment of providing all-natural cuts of beef and pork directly to our local neighborhood consumers through sustainable, natural farming practices that honor the animal and the environment.  Cattle and hogs at our ranch open graze on pesticide-free range and are never given growth hormones or antibiotics. We strive to improve the land, provide our animals with great lives, and feed you with quality food.”
710 Maitland Court, Mars, PA 16046
