We had a new, faster family favorite. If you have a big, [well-seasoned cast iron pan](https://mommypotamus.com/how-to-season-and-care-for-cast-iron-cookware/), you can probably have the whole batch done in about 15-20 minutes.\n\nMy kids love dunking these homemade chicken nuggets in this [five minute homemade ketchup recipe](https://mommypotamus.com/homemade-ketchup-recipe/) or [homemade ranch dressing](https://mommypotamus.com/homemade-ranch-dressing/). I usually add some extra veggies to their plates to dip in the ranch along with a small baked potato or something similar and dinner is done. Cleanup is easy, too!\n\n![Quick homemade chicken nuggets #paleo from 20 votes\n\nHomemade Chicken Nuggets (Gluten-Free, Paleo)\n---------------------------------------------\n\nCourse Main Course\n\nCuisine American\n\nPrep Time 5 minutes\n\nCook Time 15 minutes\n\nTotal Time 20 minutes\n\nCalories 625kcal\n\nAuthor Heather Dessinger\n\n### Equipment\n\n* large frying pan\n* sharp knife\n* small mixing bowl\n* tongs\n\n### Ingredients\n\n* 1 lb [chicken breast](https://wildpastures.com/promos/save-20-for-life/bonus15?oid=2&affid=121) (cut into 1 inch chunks)\n* 3 tbsp [arrowroot flour](https://www.amazon.com/Organic-Arrowroot-Anthonys-Gluten-Free-Non-GMO/dp/B01MYFLIA9?crid=3SUPZ14O2NHEW&keywords=organic+arrowroot&qid=1648599097&s=grocery&sprefix=organic+arrowroot%2Cgrocery%2C77&sr=1-4&linkCode=ll1&tag=mommypotamus-20&linkId=284cdfccd39cc54cb64bff49f1bdc586&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_tl)\n* 2¾ tsp [paprika](https://www.amazon.com/Simply-Organic-Paprika-Certified-Container/dp/B00269YPB8/ref=as_li_ss_tl?keywords=organic+paprika&qid=1561339185&s=gateway&sr=8-8&linkCode=ll1&tag=mommypotamus-20&linkId=fb76123e5560e88620e13d1a819def71&language=en_US)\n* ¾ tsp [unrefined sea salt](https://www.amazon.com/REDMOND-Real-Sea-Salt-Unrefined/dp/B000R5PKD0/ref=as_li_ss_tl?keywords=real+salt&qid=1561324688&s=gateway&sr=8-1-spons&th=1&linkCode=ll1&tag=mommypotamus-20&linkId=e664c2c4a55d9fd1fb9b32c5e8acae6f&language=en_US)\n* ½ tsp [onion powder](https://www.amazon.com/365-Everyday-Value-Organic-Powder/dp/B075MBH23R/ref=as_li_ss_tl?fpw=pantry&keywords=organic+onion+powder&qid=1561324093&s=pantry&sr=8-3&srs=7301146011&linkCode=ll1&tag=mommypotamus-20&linkId=ef9dfe9bf984b42294e277a991df032c&language=en_US)\n* [coconut oil](https://www.amazon.com/Nutiva-Organic-Sustainably-Coconuts-15-ounce/dp/B00NFJPK5S?dchild=1&keywords=refined+organic+coconut+oil&qid=1623848798&sr=8-5&linkCode=ll1&tag=mommypotamus-20&linkId=aece070edd4e98b7eb78deb72bed04d2&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_tl)  (for frying)\n\n### Instructions\n\n* Slice chicken breast into 1 inch chunks.\n* Combine arrowroot flour, paprika, salt and onion powder in a small bowl.\n* Add coconut oil to a large frying pan until it’s about 1/8 inch deep. Heat pan over medium-high heat.\n* One-by-one, toss the chicken pieces in the flour mixture until fully coated.\n* Add chicken to the heated frying pan using tongs. Fry until golden brown on each side and cooked through the center. I usually pull one out and cut it down the middle to check.\n* Serve with ketchup or ranch dressing.\n\n### Notes\n\n**![instagram icon](https://mommypotamus.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/58864ec6d27829db9cf6da58.png)Tried this recipe?** Mention [@mommypotamus](https://www.instagram.com/mommypotamus) or tag [#mommypotamus](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/mommypotamus)!\n\n### Nutrition\n\nCalories: 625kcal | Carbohydrates: 26g | Protein: 97g | Fat: 13g | Saturated Fat: 3g | Cholesterol: 290mg | Sodium: 2276mg | Potassium: 1823mg | Fiber: 3g | Sugar: 1g | Vitamin A: 3251IU | Vitamin C: 5mg | Calcium: 37mg | Iron: 3mg\n\n### Related Posts\n\n* [![](https://mommypotamus.com/wp-content/uploads/garlic-parmesan-chicken-tenders-210x163.jpg)](https://mommypotamus.com/paleo-chicken-tenders/)\n\n  [Garlic Parmesan Chicken Tenders (Gluten Free)](https://mommypotamus.com/paleo-chicken-tenders/)\n* [![Crispy buffalo wings on plate with ranch dressing and celery sticks](https://mommypotamus.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/buffalo-wings-recipe-oven-210x163.jpg)](https://mommypotamus.com/buffalo-wings-recipe/)\n\n  [Buffalo Wings Recipe](https://mommypotamus.com/buffalo-wings-recipe/)\n* [![](https://mommypotamus.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/paleochickenfajitaburgers-2-1.jpg)](https://mommypotamus.com/paleo-chicken-fajita-burgers/)\n\n  [Paleo Chicken Fajita Burgers](https://mommypotamus.com/paleo-chicken-fajita-burgers/)\n\n### Share with a friend!
