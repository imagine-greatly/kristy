How To Make Homemade Mayonnaise
-------------------------------

The basic recipe for mayonnaise is simple: mix together 3 room temperature egg yolks with some [salt](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/redmond?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=How+To+Make+Healthy+Mild-Tasting+Mayonnaise+%23AskWardee+128) and [raw apple cider vinegar](https://www.iherb.com/pr/Dynamic-Health-Laboratories-Organic-Raw-Apple-Cider-Vinegar-with-Mother-16-fl-oz-473-ml/85639?rcode=CVQ7401). Optionally, you can add mustard, garlic, etc. for flavor. Then, while blending or whisking\*, drizzle in 1 cup of oil(s). If all goes well, it should emulsify into mayonnaise almost like magic!

*\*Use a [stick blender](https://homedepot.sjv.io/rna4Ld), [food processor](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/cuisinartfp?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=How+To+Make+Healthy+Mild-Tasting+Mayonnaise+%23AskWardee+128), or [blender](http://tradcookschool.com/vitamix). You can whisk by hand, too; it’s harder but it still works!*

What Oils To Use For The Healthiest Mayo (Works For Trim Healthy Mama S, Keto, Paleo, Or Whole30, Too)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Any liquid oils work for mayonnaise. However, Deb asked which are the *healthiest* oils to use.

We prefer to use healthy traditional fats/oils — organic and cold- or expeller-pressed — like [extra virgin olive oil](https://wildernessfamilynaturals.com/products/olive-oil-raw-certified-organic-extra-virgin-centrifuged-filtered?ref=4), melted [coconut oil](https://wildernessfamilynaturals.com/products/cold-pressed-coconut-oil-certified-organic?ref=4), and/or [avocado oil](https://wildlyorganic.com/products/organic-avocado-oil?ref=4).

However, olive and coconut tend to impart stronger flavors into the mayonnaise. That is, unless they are more refined; more refined often equals a mild flavor. Unfortunately, though, refinement also equates to less healthy.

(Avoid the highly processed veggie oils such as canola, corn, etc. due to high processing, rancidity, and [high omega-6 fatty acid ratios](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/q-a/difference-between-grain-fed-grass-fed-meat-aw073/?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=How+To+Make+Healthy+Mild-Tasting+Mayonnaise+%23AskWardee+128). While avocado oil does contain omega-6 fatty acids, it also contains omega-3s to balance those out.)

So a really good choice for mild-tasting mayonnaise is organic cold- or expeller-pressed avocado oil. It’s actually the only oil I use for my basic mayonnaise recipe now! (Although I often add a titch of toasted sesame oil for flavor.) We really, really love it.

Homemade Mayonnaise
-------------------

Make your own more-delicious, healthier, and less expensive mayo in just a minute or so with a few common real food ingredients you probably already have in your kitchen.

I'll show you! Watch the video above to see this recipe in action.

Condiment

Prep Time 5 minutes

Author Wardee Harmon

### Ingredients

* 3
  organic or pastured egg yolks
  room temperature
* 1
  tablespoon
  [mustard](https://www.iherb.com/search?kw=organic%20dijon%20mustard&rcode=CVQ7401)
* 1
  tablespoon
  [raw apple cider vinegar](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/food-preparation/homemade-raw-apple-cider-vinegar-recipe/?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=)
* 1/2
  teaspoon
  [sea salt](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/redmond?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=)
* 1/2
  teaspoon
  [garlic powder](https://shareasale.com/r.cfm?b=140557&u=643720&m=19017&urllink=www%2Estarwest%2Dbotanicals%2Ecom%2Fcategory%2Fgarlic%2F&afftrack=)
* 1
  cup
  organic, expeller or cold pressed oils
  \* use a blend of your favorite oils

### Instructions

1. You can use a [food processor](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/cuisinartfp?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=Post_meta+with+key+wprm_instructions+for+post+Homemade+Mayonnaise+%28ID+69191%29) or [blender](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/vitamix?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=Post_meta+with+key+wprm_instructions+for+post+Homemade+Mayonnaise+%28ID+69191%29). Or a [stick blender](https://homedepot.sjv.io/rna4Ld) and a quart size jar works great.
2. Combine all ingredients, EXCEPT oil, into a quart jar or blender.
3. Blend a bit.
4. Now keep blending while slowly pouring in the cup of oil.
5. Before long (in just a minute or so)... you have mayonnaise.

### Recipe Notes

\*In the video above, I'm using [avocado](https://wildlyorganic.com/products/organic-avocado-oil?ref=4) and [grapeseed oils](https://www.iherb.com/pr/La-Tourangelle-Expeller-Pressed-Grapeseed-Oil-16-9-fl-oz-500-ml/73005?rcode=CVQ7401). I have since stopped using grapeseed oil and just use avocado oil.

What If Your Mayonnaise Doesn’t Work?
-------------------------------------

Sometimes — usually because of poor quality eggs or egg yolks that are too cold — your mayo won’t emulsify (thicken). It stays liquidy and runny, like the oil that’s added. It may even separate after being in the fridge for awhile.

It’s simple to fix this!

Get out 3 more egg yolks and let them come to room temperature. Then, while blending, slowly add the liquid mayo mixture that didn’t work. It should emulsify this time, provided you are using room temperature, good quality egg yolks.

What Do To With The Egg Whites?
-------------------------------

Egg whites are useful in lots of Trim Healthy Mama or Keto recipes… such as my [Keto Dinner Rolls](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/food-preparation/best-keto-dinner-rolls-low-carb-dairy-free-grain-free/?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=How+To+Make+Healthy+Mild-Tasting+Mayonnaise+%23AskWardee+128)!

There’s no waste when you use your yolks in sauces like mayonnaise and your whites in other dishes!

     Hi Zabeth,

     The only sure protection against salmonella/botulism is to use nitrates/nitrates. Fermenting doesn’t kill these disease causing organisms.

     Here is how to protect against salmonella and/or botulism in foods like these:

     -if fermenting, don’t ferment longer than 8 hours, then refrigerate  
     -use up within 7 to 10 days  
     -use quality eggs (local, clean, healthy, pastured)  
     -use clean kitchen equipment, etc. when preparing foods
