Lacto-Fermented Homemade Ketchup
--------------------------------

Enjoy the taste and tang of summer year round with this lacto-fermented treat -- homemade ketchup!

Course

Condiment

Author Erin VanderLugt

### Ingredients

* 12
  ounces
  tomato paste
  organic, no salt added
* 3/8
  cup
  [pure water](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/food-preparation/kitchen-tools/exploring-water-filtration-options/?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=)
* 1/8
  cup
  [whey](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/food-preparation/recipes/free-video-whey-what-it-is-how-to-get-it/?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=)
  or water\*
* 2
  tablespoons
  [raw apple cider vinegar](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/food-preparation/homemade-raw-apple-cider-vinegar-recipe/?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=)
* 1/4
  teaspoon
  ground mustard powder
  could probably use a prepared Dijon mustard if you don't have powder
* 1/8
  teaspoon
  [ground cloves](https://shareasale.com/r.cfm?b=140557&u=643720&m=19017&urllink=www%2Estarwest%2Dbotanicals%2Ecom%2Fcategory%2Fcloves%2F&afftrack=)
* 1/4
  teaspoon
  [ground cinnamon](https://shareasale.com/r.cfm?b=140557&u=643720&m=19017&urllink=www%2Estarwest%2Dbotanicals%2Ecom%2Fcategory%2Fcomfrey%2Dleaf%2F&afftrack=)
* 1/8
  teaspoon
  [ground allspice](https://shareasale.com/r.cfm?b=140557&u=643720&m=19017&urllink=www%2Estarwest%2Dbotanicals%2Ecom%2Fcategory%2Fallspice%2F&afftrack=)
* 1/8
  teaspoon
  [ground cayenne pepper](https://shareasale.com/r.cfm?b=140557&u=643720&m=19017&urllink=www%2Estarwest%2Dbotanicals%2Ecom%2Fcategory%2Fcayenne%2Dpepper%2F&afftrack=)
* 1/2
  teaspoon
  [sea salt](https://traditionalcookingschool.com/redmond?utm_campaign=autoblog&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=bloglink&utm_content=)
  I used larger grained salt
* 1/4 to 1/3
  cup
  [maple syrup](https://www.iherb.com/pr/Frontier-Natural-Products-Organic-Granulated-Maple-Syrup-16-oz-453-g/31143?rcode=CVQ7401)
  or honey or molasses

### Instructions

1. \*If using water instead of whey, increase sea salt to 3/4 teaspoon.
2. In a medium sized bowl, whisk together all ingredients.
3. Pour sauce into a storage container.
4. Cover and leave at room temperature for 2 days.
5. Move to the fridge.
