### **Why It Works Better in Your Kitchen**

- **Season with precision**


Light, coarse flakes are easy to pinch, crush, and season with control—helping chefs season consistently across every dish.
- **Superior coverage & fast flavor**


Multi-faceted crystals adhere well to food and dissolve quickly for immediate, even flavor.
- **Built for consistency on the line**


The visible flake structure acts as a natural guide—so chefs can see and control seasoning in real time.
- **Smarter sodium control**


~50% less sodium by volume than table salt, giving you flexibility without sacrificing taste.
- **One-of-a-kind crystal structure**


Made in the USA using the Alberger® process, creating a flake that delivers unmatched performance in foodservice kitchens.

### **Built for Foodservice Applications**

Designed for both prep and finishing, Diamond Crystal® Kosher Salt performs across the menu:

- Proteins (steak, poultry, seafood)
- Vegetables and sides
- Sauces, soups, and scratch recipes
- Signature seasonings and blends
- Rimming cocktails, mocktails and other on-trend beverages
- Finishing and plating
