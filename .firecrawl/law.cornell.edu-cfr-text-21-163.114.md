# 21 CFR § 163.114 - Lowfat cocoa.

§ 163.114 Lowfat cocoa.

(a)Description. Lowfat cocoa is the [food](https://www.law.cornell.edu/definitions/index.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=5f3b7ccf4b3f4f15bfde1fba6edad879&term_occur=999&term_src=Title:21:Chapter:I:Subchapter:B:Part:163:Subpart:B:163.114) that conforms to the definition and standard of identity, and is subject to the requirements for label declaration of ingredients for breakfast cocoa in [§ 163.112](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/163.112), except that the cacao fat content is less than 10 percent by weight, as determined by the method prescribed in [§ 163.5(b)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/163.5#b).

(b)Nomenclature. The name of the food is “lowfat cocoa”.
