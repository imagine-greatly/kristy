# FSIS Guideline on Substantiating Animal-Raising or Environment-Related Labeling Claims

Guideline IDFSIS-GD-2024-0006

Issue DateAugust 2024

Full Guideline

[FSIS Guideline on Substantiating Animal-Raising or Environment-Related Labeling Claims](https://www.usda.gov/guidance-documents/labeling/fsis/fsis-guideline-substantiating-animal-raising-or-environment-related-labeling-claims)

* * *

**Replaces:** December 2019 Version of the Guideline: FSIS-GD-2019-0009

This guideline provides establishments with information on how to use and substantiate animal-raising or environment-related claims on meat or poultry product labels. It relates to 9 CFR 412.2, 9 CFR part 317, and 9 CFR 381 subpart N.
