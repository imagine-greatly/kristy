# Organic Dairy Scorecard

[Home](https://www.cornucopia.org/ "Go to Cornucopia Institute.")/[Scorecards](https://www.cornucopia.org/scorecards/ "Go to Scorecards.")/Organic Dairy Scorecard

[Back to Scorecards](https://www.cornucopia.org/scorecards/)

Consult our Organic Dairy Scorecard to find the highest-rated brands you can afford. When you can’t find top-rated brands (the 4s and 5s), do the best you can: purchasing any organic dairy brand on our scorecard, even those at the bottom, is a vote for a food system free from the most toxic agrochemicals.

Purchasing authentic organic is an investment in healthy systems — and one way to resist the industrialization of organic dairy. Use our Organic Dairy Scorecard to support authentic organic dairy farms. And choose local, organic dairy if you can! Read our detailed breakdown of the [Organic Dairy Scorecard criteria](https://www.cornucopia.org/organic-dairy-scorecard-criteria/).
