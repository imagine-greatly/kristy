# Interface Identity — Cart-Centered, Two Equal Tools, Scanner Dethroned

The interface must reflect "complete food coach," not "scanner with a list." The fix is not a store-map rebuild. It's: the cart is the center (the shopper's unit of intent), and the two ways to fill it — Scan (labeled) and Counter (unlabeled) — become visual PEERS, with the scanner demoted from its center-throne. That structure IS "coach for the whole store, labeled and not." Standing rules: claim lock, VOICE_SPEC (egoless, no first person, no em-dash, brief), one commit per block.

---

## THE PRINCIPLE

The cart is the sun. Scanning, the counter, and the haul orbit it. Scanning vets packaged things FOR the cart; the counter answers questions about unpackaged things going IN the cart; the haul reads how the cart came out. So the app opens on the CART (the trip = the shopper's actual intent), and the two ways to fill it are co-equal. Neither the scanner (that's Yuka) nor a store-map (that's the store's logic, not the shopper's) is the center. The cart is.

---

## BLOCK 1 — Dethrone the scanner; make Scan and Counter equal peers

```
The scanner is currently the giant gold center-throne of the nav, which makes the app read as "a scanner" no matter what the copy says. Structure defines identity. Fix the hierarchy so the interface says "coach for the whole cart, labeled and not."

1) THE APP OPENS ON THE CART. The cart/trip is home — the default surface on open (for a user with preferences; a fresh user gets the chat prompt that builds the cart, per the user-built-list work). Not the scanner, not a menu.

2) KILL THE SCANNER THRONE. Remove the oversized gold center scan button as the visual hero. Scanning stays instantly reachable but becomes a PEER, not the throne.

3) SCAN AND COUNTER ARE VISUAL EQUALS. The two ways to fill/vet the cart:
   - SCAN — the packaged half (barcode / label photo).
   - COUNTER — the unlabeled half (meat, fish, eggs, produce, bulk — Kristy's sourced guidance).
   Present them at EQUAL weight in the nav/surfaces. Same size, same prominence. This equality IS the positioning: the labeled and unlabeled halves matter equally, and the unlabeled half is the thing no scanner can do.

4) NAV SHAPE: Cart (home) · Scan · Counter · Haul — with Scan and Counter as equal peers, Cart as the center/home, Haul as the after. No single throne. If the current nav has a raised center button, either remove that treatment or make it neutral.

5) THE CART SURFACE shows the trip taking shape, chat-editable (per the user-built-list work), with both "scan something" and "ask the counter" reachable as equal actions from the cart.

Verify: the app opens on the cart; the scanner is no longer the oversized center-throne; Scan and Counter render at equal visual weight as the two ways to fill the cart; nav is Cart(home)/Scan/Counter/Haul with no single dominant throne.
```

## BLOCK 2 — The Counter as a real destination (equal to Scan)

```
The Counter (unlabeled-store coaching) must feel like a first-class destination equal to scanning — because it's the moat. Make it a place a shopper goes, not a buried link.

1) COUNTER IS BROWSABLE BY SECTION: Produce, Meat, Seafood, Dairy & Eggs, Pantry & Bulk. Tap a section, get Kristy's sourced guidance for that part of the store (what to look for, how to pick, label truth, buying tips). This is the deepened perimeter knowledge (from the perimeter-depth work) made into a navigable surface.

2) EQUAL FOOTING WITH SCAN: opening the Counter is as easy and prominent as opening the scanner. Standing at the fish case with nothing to scan = one tap to Kristy's fish guidance, same as one tap to scan a box.

3) IT ALSO ANSWERS CONVERSATIONALLY: the composer takes a counter question ("wild or farmed", "which cut for stew", "is organic worth it for berries") from anywhere and routes to the perimeter answer. Browse OR ask, both first-class.

4) FEEDS THE CART: a counter recommendation ("pasture-raised eggs", "wild sardines") can be added to the cart in one tap — so the counter isn't just reference, it fills the cart like scanning does.

5) COPY per VOICE_SPEC: egoless, no first person, brief. The counter's framing: "The half of the store with no label. The half that matters most." Short.

Verify: the Counter is a browsable, first-class destination equal to Scan; sections give real sourced guidance; the composer answers counter questions from anywhere; counter picks add to the cart in one tap; copy is brief and egoless.
```

## Note for Devon — why this and not a store-map

I considered the store-sections-as-spine model and rejected it: a store map is organized around the STORE's logic, not the shopper's. Nobody opens the app wanting to "browse the meat section" — they open it needing to feed themselves well this week. Opening on a store map makes the user do the navigating a coach should remove. Beautiful, backwards.

The cart is the shopper's real unit of intent — the whole trip, everything they're trying to accomplish. Everything Kristy does serves the cart. So the cart is the center, and the two ways to fill it (Scan = labeled, Counter = unlabeled) become co-equal tools. That equality is the entire identity: coach for the whole store, both halves, neither dominant, and the unlabeled half — the moat — finally at equal billing instead of buried under a scanner throne.

The mistake was never the cart being central (it was right). It was the scanner sitting on a throne beside it, screaming "Yuka." Dethrone the scanner, raise the counter to Scan's equal, and the interface finally says what Kristy is.
